Yuchen Water Vontron mai rabawa ne kuma dillali. Muna ba da kayan membrane don ayyukan zama, kasuwanci da masana'antu kuma muna ba da tallafin fasaha na kayan aiki masu alaƙa. Rukuni na membrane ta hanyar aikin jiyya

Rukunin membrane suna bayyana matsayin rabuwa. Ba su zama masu maye gurbin rahoton ruwa ba ko shawarwarin abu na ƙarshe ba.

Rukuni na membrane ta hanyar aikin jiyya

Juya osmosis (RO)

Ana la'akari da RO lokacin da ake buƙatar raguwar gishiri-gishiri mai faɗi ko ruwan samfur mafi girma. Ciyar da sinadarai, matsa lamba, pretreatment da kuma mayar da hankali yanayi suna bayyana iyaka mai amfani.

Rukuni na membrane ta hanyar aikin jiyya

Nanofiltration (NF)

NF can support selective dissolved-species and organic separation. Dole ne a bayyana maƙasudin da aka riƙe da masu ratsawa, pH, zafin jiki da yanayin tsaftacewa.

Rukuni na membrane ta hanyar aikin jiyya

Ultrafiltration (UF) da MBR

Matsayin UF da MBR yawanci suna mayar da hankali kan abubuwan da aka dakatar, colloidal da kayan halitta. Zasu iya zama shinge na ƙarshe ko pretreatment kafin matakan membrane mai ƙarfi.

Rukuni na membrane ta hanyar aikin jiyya

SW Series da ruwan gishiri RO

Ruwan teku da aikace-aikacen ruwa na brackish suna buƙatar salinity, ion, zafin jiki, ci, pretreatment da babban matsi-kayan bita kafin zaɓin kayan.

Hanyoyin aikace-aikace

Fara daga tushen da nufin amfani. Kowace hanya tana kaiwa ga lissafin rahoton rahoto da tattaunawa ta fasaha, ba zuwa zaɓin ƙirar atomatik ba.

Ruwan ruwa da ruwa mai laushi

Yi bitar salinity, ions, zafin jiki, bambancin sha, haɗarin ilimin halitta, raɗaɗin manufa da ƙuntatawa.

Ruwan karkashin kasa da ruwan rijiya

Bincika taurin, alkalinity, silica, baƙin ƙarfe, manganese, gas, salinity da gurɓataccen gida.

Municipal da shiri na ruwan sha

Tabbatar da magungunan kashe kwayoyin cuta, kwayoyin halitta, tauri, maƙasudin dandano, kayan da ake buƙata da amfani da ƙasa.

Sake amfani da ruwan sharar masana'antu

Bayyana tsarin samarwa, sauye-sauye, kwayoyin halitta, mai, karafa, daskararru, sake amfani da makoma da tattara hankali.

Babban-salinity da tallafi masu alaƙa da ZLD

Samar da cikakkiyar ma'auni na ion, ƙananan haɗarin sikelin, kwayoyin halitta, sauye-sauye da rawar da ake nufi na matakin membrane.

Gishiri-lake brine da ruwan da ke da alaƙa da lithium

Fadi cikakken abun da ke ciki da makasudin rabuwa. Tallafin membrane-material ba alƙawarin cikakken tsarin cirewa ba ne ko sakamakon farfadowa.

Abinci, abin sha, magunguna da ruwaye na musamman

Ƙayyade abubuwan da aka riƙe, maƙasudin ingancin samfur, tsafta, pH, zafin jiki, tsaftacewa da buƙatun shaida.

Kasuwancin wurin zama da haske

Daidaita ruwan shigar da ruwa, buƙatu, matsa lamba, gine-gine mai tsarkakewa, riga-kafi da samun damar maye gurbin maimakon alamar iya aiki.

  • cikakken rahoton dakin gwaje-gwaje na yanzu

Me yasa Yuchen Water ke neman rahoton ruwa kafin bayar da shawarar membrane?

A'a. Yana taimaka wa masu siye su shirya ingantaccen bayani; Shawarar ƙarshe ta biyo bayan rahoto da duba yanayin aiki.

Shin Yuchen Water na iya ba da tallafin kayan aiki da kayan membrane?

Ee. Babban wadata shine kayan aikin membrane, tare da goyon bayan da suka danganci pretreatment, gidaje, famfo, tacewa, sarrafawa, jagorar ƙaddamarwa da shirin maye gurbin.

Ka'idojin RO da NF membrane: hana gishiri, flux, recovery da osmotic pressure

Ba a iya tantance aikin RO/NF da TDS ko kaso guda na hana gishiri kawai ba. A duba rejection, salt passage, yawan permeate, flux, recovery, zafin jiki, matsin lamba, sinadaran ruwan feed da sharuddan gwajin membrane tare. Kimar element daya ba ta bada tabbacin ingancin permeate na dukkan tsarin ba.

Ma'anoni da misalai

Ana lissafa observed salt rejection kamar haka: (1 − concentration na permeate ÷ concentration na feed) × 100%. Salt passage = 100% − rejection. Flux shi ne permeate flow ÷ active membrane area; recovery shi ne permeate flow ÷ feed flow. A rubuta inda aka dauki samfur, hanyar aunawa, yanayin aiki da ko an yi normalization.

Ga dilute ideal solution, π = RTΣCi kimanta ce ta ilimi, tare da daidaitattun units. Misalin supplier: 2 g/L NaCl a 25°C, C≈0.0342 mol/L da π≈169 kPa (1.69 bar). Wannan ba cikakken samfurin seawater activity ba. Teburin tushe ya yi kuskuren rubuta NaCl 34.2 da MgSO₄ 16.7 a mol/L; bisa molecular weights da 2,000 mg/L, units masu dacewa mmol/L ne (0.0342 da 0.0167 mol/L), ba mol/L ba.

Misalin LP22-8040 yana da 99.7% stable rejection, 10,500 GPD da 400 ft²; lissafi ya bada 26.25 GFD. Waɗannan kimomi na wannan model ne kawai, kuma sai an kwatanta su a sharuddan gwajin da aka bayyana. Ba sa tabbatar da aikin shuka.

Yanayin aiki da iyaka

Ingantaccen pressure mafi girma na iya ƙara water flux; zafi yana sauya viscosity da jigilar gishiri; salinity na feed yana ƙara osmotic pressure. Concentration polarization na iya sa concentration a saman membrane ta fi ta babban ruwan feed. Tasirin ya danganta da membrane, tsarin element, recovery da hanyar gwaji.

Don bita mai amfani, a bayar da cikakken water analysis, ingancin feed da target permeate, flow da ake bukata, temperature, pH, recovery, operating pressure, pretreatment da normalized trend. TDS kadai ba ya nuna scaling ions, silica, organics, oxidants ko fouling risk. Wannan kayan ilimi ne bisa bayanan fasaha na supplier da ka'idojin membrane; ba izinin design ko umarnin aiki ba ne. A tabbatar da takamaiman model da yanayin wurin.

Tambayoyi

Shin 99.7% rejection na element yana nufin na shuka ma 99.7%?

A'a. Sakamako ya danganta da feed, aiki, shekarun element, staging, seals, blending da aunawa.

TDS kadai zai zabi RO ko NF?

A'a; ion composition, target quality, recovery da scaling suna da muhimmanci.

Flux da permeate capacity iri daya ne?

A'a. Flux ana daidaita shi da membrane area; capacity kuma ya danganta da area da tsarin aiki.

Tsarin masana'antar RO: recovery, concentration factor da staging

Recovery = permeate flow ÷ feed flow. Idan recovery ya karu, concentrate flow yana raguwa amma salinity na concentrate na karuwa, tare da yiwuwar scaling, concentration polarization da hadarin aiki. A lissafta recovery daga cikakken feed analysis, target flow, membrane model, zafi da iyakokin stages; kada a zabi generic rule.

Mass balance da concentration factor

A steady state: feed = permeate + concentrate. Idan aka yi watsi da salt passage da precipitation, kimanta concentration factor = 1/(1 − recovery): 75% → 4×; 80% → 5×; 90% → 10×. Misalan lissafi ne, ba design prediction ba. A tantance salt passage, precipitation, antiscalant, temperature, staging da recovery limits.

Misalin supplier ya bada feed 80 m³/h, concentrate 20 m³/h da permeate 60 m³/h, wato 75% recovery. Yin balance da TDS na feed/permeate/concentrate gwajin diagnostic ne kawai idan samfurori wakilai ne, shuka tana stable, meters calibrated kuma concentrations ana auna su ta hanya mai kama. TDS ba ya wakiltar kowane scaling ion daidai.

Iyakar design case da bayanan da ake bukata

Misalin groundwater design software a supplier material shafuka 33–45 ya nuna 100 m³/h permeate, 25°C, 75% recovery; kuma 27 LMH flux, kusan 100 elements, da 2.5 bar interstage boost a optimization example. Ba a samu cikakken water analysis, software input/output ko membrane configuration domin sake tabbatarwa ba. A dauki lambobin a matsayin tarihin misali kawai, ba shawarar design ba.

Engineer yana bukatar cikakken ionic analysis, ciki har da hardness, alkalinity, sulfate, silica, barium/strontium, iron da organics idan ya dace; canjin feed, permeate target, flow, temperature, pH, pretreatment, model, pressure vessels, makamashi, concentrate disposal limits da standards. TDS guda daya bai isa ba.

A'a. Ana bukatar individual ions, pH, temperature da concentrate concentration.

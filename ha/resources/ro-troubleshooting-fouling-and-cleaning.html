Binciken matsalar RO: gano fouling kafin cleaning

Permeate flow da ya ragu, differential pressure da ya karu ko salt passage da ya canza alamomi ne, ba diagnosis ba. Da farko tabbatar meters da samfurori; kwatanta normalized performance da valid baseline; duba canjin feed/pretreatment; sannan gano foulant. A yi cleaning ne kawai bisa current membrane/system cleaning guide, sinadaran da aka amince da su da safety controls.

Binciken alamomi

Low flow na iya zuwa daga karancin temperature, pressure ko feed flow, recovery da ya canza, fouling, scaling, instrument error ko permeate backpressure. High stage differential pressure na iya nuna feed-channel blockage, solids, biofouling ko hydraulic imbalance. Salt passage da ya karu na iya zuwa daga feed/temperature change, oxidation, seal leakage, damage ko measurement error. Dalilai suna iya hadewa; kada a yanke hukunci da indicator guda.

A tattara normalized permeate flow da salt passage; pressures by stage; feed/concentrate/permeate flows da conductivity; temperature, pH, recovery, SDI/turbidity; pretreatment chemical records, alarms, cleaning history da chemistry na feed/concentrate. Duba calibration da yanayi iri daya.

Iyakar hujja da tsaftacewa

Shafi 62 na gabatarwar supplier ya rubuta failure split 4% mechanical, 18% degradation da 78% fouling. Ba a bada denominator, sample, method, period ko current applicability ba; historical unverified supplier sample ne kawai, ba general probability ba.

Presentation ta ambaci normalized permeate decline >15%, salt-passage rise >10% ko feed-to-concentrate pressure-drop rise >15% a matsayin cleaning indicators. Current public quality terms suna cewa system performance decrease 10% under standard conditions na bukatar cleaning bisa specified procedure. Waɗannan thresholds sun bambanta; kada a hada su ko dauke su universal ba. Kada a sake amfani da generic acid/alkali/chelant/surfactant recipe; compatibility ya danganta da model, foulant, mixing order, temperature, pH da kayan system. Sinadari mara dacewa na iya lalata element ko cutar da ma'aikata.

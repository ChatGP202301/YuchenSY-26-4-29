Shigarwa da commissioning na RO membrane: jerin tabbatarwa mai aminci

Kafin shigarwa ko fara RO, tabbatar da exact membrane model, pressure-vessel arrangement, current membrane-manufacturer instructions, system OEM procedure, readiness na pretreatment, instruments da site safety controls. Bayanin matakai na gama-gari ba ya maye gurbin manual na yanzu ko SOP da aka amince da shi.

Shiri da handling

Supplier material ya tattauna tsaftataccen handling, duba pressure vessel da internal components, tabbatar seals da element orientation, da hana contamination ko mechanical damage. A tsabtace housing da ruwa kafin loading, a daidaita seals. Lubricant/material compatibility da loading order sun dogara da model da tsarin; duba takamaiman umarni, kada a yi zato.

Kafin startup da commissioning

Tabbatar pretreatment yana cikin current membrane limits; duba feed chemistry, pH, temperature, turbidity/SDI, oxidant control, pressure gauges, flow meters, valves, concentrate path da permeate diversion. Cire trapped air kuma kauce wa water hammer bisa approved procedure. Ruwa mai bayyana ko TDS daya ba ya nuna startup readiness.

Yi amfani da startup guide na exact model da system wajen saita pressure, flow, recovery da flushing. Rubuta feed/permeate/concentrate flows; stage pressures; temperature; pH; conductivity/TDS da measurement method; da normalization basis. Kafa baseline bayan aiki ya tsaya a documented conditions. Kada a dauki tsoffin time/pressure figures a matsayin universal instructions.

Idan readings sun fita daga approved startup range, instruments ba su yarda ba, akwai leakage ko pretreatment/oxidant control bai tabbata ba, tsaya bisa plant SOP kuma tuntubi responsible system engineer. Kada a yi chemical clean ko canza recovery domin boye matsala da ba a fahimta ba. Wannan educational checklist ne bisa supplier technical material; ba ya maye gurbin model manual, chemical safety data ko site permit/SOP.

Tambayoyi

Za a iya amfani da wannan jerin a matsayin startup procedure?

A'a. Bi exact membrane da system instructions da SOP.

Za a iya hada models daban-daban a vessel daya?

Sai system designer da manufacturer documentation sun tabbatar da compatibility.

Me za a rubuta a commissioning?

Approved conditions, calibrated measurements, stable baseline, deviations da corrective actions.

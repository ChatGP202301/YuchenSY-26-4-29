Ukufaka Nokunika I-RO Membrane Igunya Lokusebenza: Uhlu Lokuhlola Ukuphepha

Ngaphambi kokufaka noma ukuqala isistimu ye-RO, qinisekisa imodeli ye-membrane eqondile, ukuhlelwa kwe-pressure vessel, imiyalelo yamanje yomkhiqizi we-membrane, inqubo ye-system OEM, ukulungela kwe-pretreatment, amathuluzi nezilawuli zokuphepha zendawo. Amanothi ajwayelekile okulandelana awathathi indawo yemanuwali yamanje noma ye-SOP yesitshalo egunyaziwe.

Ukulungiselela nokuphatha

Impahla yomphakeli ihlanganisa ukuphatha okuhlanzekile, ukuhlola i-pressure vessel nezinto zangaphakathi, ukuqinisekisa ama-seal nokuma kwe-element, nokugwema ukungcola noma ukulimala kwemishini. Umhlahlandlela wamanje wokunakekela i-membrane ugcizelela nokugeza i-housing ngaphambi kokulayisha nokubeka ama-seal ngendlela efanele. Ukuhambisana kwe-seal lubricant/material nokulandelana kokulayisha kuncike kumodeli nasesistimuni; qinisekisa imiyalelo yangempela kunokuba uzenzele.

Ngaphambi kokuqala

Qinisekisa ukuthi i-pretreatment isebenza ngaphakathi kwemikhawulo yamanje ye-element; hlola i-feed chemistry, i-pH, izinga lokushisa, turbidity/SDI nokulawulwa kwama-oxidant. Hlola ama-pressure instrument, ama-flow meter, ama-valve, indlela ye-concentrate nokuphambukiswa kwe-permeate. Khipha umoya ovalelekile futhi ugweme i-water hammer ngokwenqubo yesistimu egunyaziwe. Amanzi abonakala ecacile noma ukufundwa kwe-TDS okukodwa akufakazeli ukuthi sekulungele ukuqala.

Ukuqalisa nokusungula i-baseline

Sebenzisa umhlahlandlela wokuqalisa wemodeli nesistimu eqondile ukuze usethe ingcindezi, ukugeleza, i-recovery nokugeza. Bhala ukugeleza kwamanzi angenayo, i-permeate ne-concentrate; ingcindezi ngesigaba; izinga lokushisa; i-pH; i-conductivity/TDS nendlela yokulinganisa; kanye nesisekelo se-normalization. Sungula i-baseline kuphela ngemva kokusebenza okuzinzile ngaphansi kwezimo ezibhaliwe. Ungasebenzisi isikhathi noma ingcindezi evela kuphrezentheshini endala njengomyalelo ojwayelekile.

Misa bese uqinisekisa

Uma ukufundwa kuphuma ku-startup envelope egunyaziwe, amathuluzi engavumelani, kuvuza, noma ukulawulwa kwe-pretreatment/oxidant kungaqinisekile, yima ngokwe-SOP yesitshalo bese uthintana nonjiniyela wesistimu onesibopho. Ungenzi chemical clean noma ushintshe i-recovery ukuze ufihle okungajwayelekile okungachazwanga.

Imikhawulo nesinyathelo esilandelayo

Lolu hlu lokufundisa lusekelwe emininingwaneni yobuchwepheshe bomphakeli nasemhlahlandlela wokunakekela ovulekile wamanje; aluthathi indawo yemanuwali yemodeli ye-membrane, imiyalelo ye-pressure vessel, imibhalo ye-system OEM, i-chemical safety data noma imvume/SOP yendawo. I-Yuchen Water ingasiza ukuthola imibhalo yobuchwepheshe nedatha yokusebenza edingekayo ekubuyekezweni kwe-membrane/material. Nikeza imodeli, umdwebo wesistimu, ukuhlaziywa kwamanzi angenayo kanye nebanga lokusebenza elihlosiwe.

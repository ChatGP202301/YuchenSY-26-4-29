Impendulo eqondile

Ukushintshwa kwesihlungi sekhabhoni kufanele kuqaliswe isisekelo sesevisi esigunyaziwe, hhayi inombolo eyodwa yendawo yonke yezinyanga. Amasignali ahambisanayo angabandakanya impumelelo yenhlanganisela eqondiwe, ukulahlekelwa indawo yokugcina yezinzwa egunyaziwe, ivolumu ephathwayo, ukuncipha kwengcindezi, ukushintsha kokugeleza, isimo semidiya, imicimbi yokukhucululwa kwendle noma imiyalelo eqinisekisiwe yomkhiqizo oqediwe oqinisekisiwe. Ukuxazulula inkinga kuqala ngokuhlukanisa ukukhathala kwe-adsorption kusukela ekuvinjweni kwamanzi, umzila, ukudlula, izinhlawulo, ukukhula kwebhayoloji, iphutha lesampula kanye nezinguquko zamanzi akhuphuka nomfula. Yuchen Water ingabuyekeza umbiko wamanzi, umlando wokusebenza, i-cartridge noma imininingwane yomkhumbi kanye namarekhodi okuqapha ukuze asekele indawo esetshenziswayo nokushintshwa, kodwa isikhathi esiqondile sesevisi sidinga ubufakazi bamanzi angempela, ukugeleza nokwakhiwa.

Qala ngenkinga yomthengi

Isikhalazo esifana nokubuyisa iphunga singabonisa impumelelo, kodwa futhi singase sibangele umcimbi omusha wamanzi omthombo, ukungcoliswa komfula, ukudlula noma inkinga yesampula. Ukugeleza okuphansi kungase kubonise ukuvinjelwa esikhundleni somthamo we-adsorption ophelile. Izinhlayiya ezimnyama zingase ziqhamuke ekugezelweni okunganele kokuqala, izingxenye zokugcina ezilimele noma ukuphazamiseka kombhede. Ukuphatha zonke izimpawu njengokwehluleka okufanayo kumosha amakhatriji futhi kungashiya ingozi yesistimu yangempela ingaxazululiwe.

Ngakho-ke irekhodi lokusebenza liyingxenye yobufakazi bomkhiqizo. Rekhoda idethi yokufaka, imidiya noma i-cartridge identity, indawo lapho itholakala khona, ukugungiswa kokuqala, ukunqwabelana kwevolumu yokwelashwa okukhulayo, isilinganiso nokugeleza okuphezulu, ingcindezi ngaphambi nangemuva kwesiteji, imiphumela yokuhlaziya, ukukhucululwa kwendle kanye nemicimbi yomthombo engajwayelekile. Uhlelo oluwusizo lokumiselela luguqula lawa marekhodi abe umthetho wesenzo futhi lugcine izinto ezisele zitholakala ngaphambi kokuthi kufinyelelwe isibangeli.

Imuphi umbiko nedatha yokusebenza edingekayo

Thumela kokubili idatha yamanzi yamanje kanye nomlando wokuhluleka. Izithombe zingasekela ukubuyekezwa kokwakhiwa, kodwa azifaki esikhundleni sekhemistri, ukugeleza, ingcindezi kanye nolwazi lwesampula.

  • umbiko wamanzi omthombo kanye nanoma yiluphi ushintsho kusukela kwafakwa ikhabhoni
  • inhlanganisela eqondiwe noma iphoyinti lokugcina nangaphambi/ngemuva kwemiphumela yokuhlaziya
  • usuku lokufakwa, i-cartridge noma ubunikazi bemidiya, inkatho nevolumu yokwelashwa uma kwaziwa
  • ukugeleza okumaphakathi nokuphakeme, isikhathi sokusebenza sansuku zonke kanye nezikhathi zokuma
  • ingcindezi yokungena nokuphumayo noma inkambiso yokucindezela okuhlukile
  • amarekhodi okugudluzwa kokuqala, ukuthuthwa kwendle, ukuwashwa ngemuva namanye amarekhodi okugcina
  • isimo se-sediment ekhuphukayo kanye nezimpawu zemishini engezansi
  • izindawo zamasampula, indlela, isikhathi kanye nomthetho wokushintsha osetshenziswayo njengamanje

Umbiko kufanele ube wakamuva ngokwanele ukuze umelele umthombo ohlongozwayo futhi kufanele uhlonze idethi yesampula nendawo. Uma amanzi eshintsha ngesizini, inqwaba yokukhiqiza noma isimo sokusebenza, hlinzeka ngobubanzi kunomphumela owodwa olula. Yisho inhloso yamanzi ahlanzekile adingekayo ngokwehlukana nokuhlaziywa kwamanje. Uma inani lingaziwa, limaka njengelingaziwa; igebe elibonakalayo liphephile kunenombolo eqageliwe.

Ulwazi lwe-hydraulic luyingxenye yekhemistri. Ukugeleza okumaphakathi kukodwa kungafihla izimo ezimfushane eziphakeme, umsebenzi wezikhawu kanye nokuma okude. Qinisekisa isikhathi sokusebenza sansuku zonke, isidingo esiphezulu, ingcindezi etholakalayo, ukulahlekelwa kwengcindezi okuvunyelwe, ubukhulu bemishini futhi WHO izoqapha futhi imiselele ikhabhoni. Le mininingwane inquma ukuthi imidiya ebambekayo ingasetshenziswa yini kusistimu esebenzisekayo.

I-logic yokukhetha

Uma inhlanganisela eqondiwe ivela ngemva kwekhabhoni kuyilapho ukugeleza nokucindezela kuhlala kuyinto evamile, phenya ukuphumelela, ukulayishwa komthombo kanye namasampula kuqala. Qinisekisa ukuthi ukugxilwa kwe-inlet nezingxenye eziqhudelanayo akukashintshi, ukuthi amasampula amele izimbobo ezilungile nokuthi indlela yokuhlaziya ifanelekile. Qhathanisa umphumela nezinga lesenzo okuvunyelwene ngaso; ungalindi ushintsho olubonakalayo noma lwezinzwa lapho okuqondiwe kungakwazi ukutholwa ngokwethembeka ngabasebenzisi.

Uma umfutho ohlukile ukhuphuka noma ukugeleza kwehla, hlola isilawuli sezinto eziqinile ezikhuphuka nomfula, ukuvinjelwa kwekhatriji, ukuminyana kombhede kanye nokuma kwevalvu. Ukushintsha imidiya ye-adsorption ngaphandle kokulungisa ukulayisha okuqinile kungakhiqiza ukwehluleka ngokushesha. Uma ingcindezi iphansi ngokungavamile ngokuphathwa kabi, hlola i-bypass, ama-seal, abasabalalisi bangaphakathi, iziteshi noma imidiya engekho. Ukuhlolwa kwemikhumbi kanye nezinqubo zokuhlukaniswa ngokuphephile kufanele zilandele ukwakheka kwemishini.

Uma kuvela izinhlawulo, buyekeza ukucaciswa kwezinhlayiyana zekhabhoni, izikrini zokugcina, umonakalo wokufakwa, ezokuthutha, izimo zokugungiswa kanye nezimo zokuwashwa ngemuva. Uma iphunga noma ukukhathazeka nge-microbiological kulandela ukumiswa, buyekeza ukuthuthwa kwendle, izinga lokushisa, isikhathi sokuphumula kanye nokungcoliswa komfula. Ikhabhoni ecushiwe ayilona uhlelo lokubulala amagciwane, futhi isigaba esikhathele noma esinganakekelwa kahle akumele sishiywe emsebenzini ngenxa nje yokuthi ukucindezela kuhlala kwamukelekile.

Ukwelashwa kwangaphambili, okokusebenza kanye nesimo sokusebenza

Uhlelo olushintshayo kufanele lusho isiqalisi esivamile, umphumela oyisixwayiso kusengaphambili, umkhawulo wesevisi ovunyelwe uma usekelwa, umuntu onomthwalo wemfanelo kanye nesenzo lapho idatha ingekho. Imibhede ye-lead-lag yezimboni ingase isebenzise ukuqapha kanye nokuzungezisa indawo, kuyilapho izinhlelo ze-cartridge zingasebenzisa umthamo olashiwe, imiyalelo eqinisekisiwe nokuhlola ngezikhathi ezithile. Isikhathi esifanayo akufanele sikopishwe phakathi kwezindawo ezinekhwalithi yamanzi ehlukile kanye nesidingo.

Ngemuva kokushintsha, bhala phansi imidiya entsha noma i-cartridge, uyishaye noma uyithumele ngokwenqubo egunyaziwe, buyisela isampula lesisekelo bese uqinisekisa ingcindezi kanye nekhwalithi yamanzi. Ukwehluleka kwangaphambi kwesikhathi okuphindaphindiwe kuwubufakazi bokuphinde uvakashele isitimela sokwelapha, hhayi ukumane ufinyeze ikhalenda. Yuchen Water ingasekela ukunikezwa kwezinto ezishintshayo, ukuhlola ukuhambisana kanye nokuxazululwa kwezinkinga kwemishini evela kumarekhodi athunyelwe.

Amaphutha ajwayelekile

  • Ukusebenzisa isikhawu esisodwa sokushintsha kuwo wonke umthombo wamanzi nokugeleza
  • Ukuthola ukukhathala kwe-adsorption kusuka ekulahlekelweni kwengcindezi kuphela
  • Ukushintsha ikhabhoni ngaphandle kokuhlola izinto eziqinile ezikhuphuka nomfula noma i-bypass yesistimu
  • Ukunganaki ukumila, ukukhucululwa kwendle nezimo zebhayoloji
  • Yehlulekile ukurekhoda indawo efakiwe, ivolumu ephathwayo nangaphambi/ngemuva kwamasampuli

Ubufakazi kanye nomngcele wesevisi

Alikho ikhasi noma ikhotheshini okufanele ithembise impilo yesevisi ngaphandle kokuchaza amanzi, okuhlosiwe, imidiya, ukwakhiwa, ukugeleza nendawo yokugcina. Umyalelo womkhiqizi oqinisekisiwe womkhiqizo oqediwe kufanele ulandelwe kulowo mkhiqizo; ayidluliseki ngokuzenzakalelayo enguqulweni ye-OEM. Izikhawu zezimboni kufanele zisekelwe ngokuqapha, ubufakazi obufanele noma ukuqagela kwedizayini okufanelekile.

Ukubuyekezwa kobuchwepheshe kungakhomba izindlela zokuhluleka okungenzeka, idatha engekho nezinketho zokumiselela, kodwa ukubuyekezwa okukude akukwazi ukuthatha indawo yokuhlolwa kwesayithi okuphephile. Umsebenzisi uhlala enesibopho sokuhlukaniswa, ukuphatha, ukukhucululwa kwendle, ukuthatha amasampula kanye nokubuyela kusevisi. Yuchen Water Ububanzi bezinto ezibonakalayo kanye nokwesekwa kwemishini okusekelwe olwazini olutholiwe.

Yuchen Water ibuyekeza okuhlosiwe kanye nobufakazi kuqala, bese ihlonza ifomethi yekhabhoni efanele kanye nemibuzo ebalulekile, esekela ukwelapha kwangaphambili nezidingo zemishini, kanye namadokhumenti noma izivivinyo ezisadingeka. Okukhiphayo kungaba isiqondiso sokuqala, isicelo solwazi olungekho, noma isincomo sento esekelayo nesisetshenziswa. Isincomo asikhishwa ngoba nje umthengi unikeze igama lomkhiqizo.

Ngemuva kokuhlinzekwa, ukwesekwa kwezobuchwepheshe kungafaka ukuqinisekiswa kwedokhumenti, imibuzo yokufaka kanye ne-flushing, isiqondiso sokusebenza esikude, izindawo zokuqapha kanye nokuhlelwa kokushintsha ngaphakathi kwendawo okuvunyelwene ngayo. Ukusebenza kwangempela kusancike ezintweni ezigunyaziwe, okokusebenza okuphelele, amanzi omthombo, ukufakwa, ukusebenza nokugcinwa. Isiqondiso esishicilelwe asikwazi ukufaka esikhundleni sokubuyekezwa kwephrojekthi.

Akukho lutho lokugcina, umthamo, impilo yesevisi, iphesenti lokususwa noma umphumela wephrojekthi othenjiswayo ngaphambi kokubuyekezwa kombiko, ukwakhiwa, ubufakazi kanye nezimo zokusebenza.

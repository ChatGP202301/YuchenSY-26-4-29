Uyifunda Kanjani I-Carbon Raw-Material Esebenzayo kanye Nezicaciso Zokuhlola

Funda ukuthi izicaciso zekhabhoni esekwe emalahleni, inombolo ye-iodine, i-mesh, umlotha, umswakama nobulukhuni kufanele zifundwe kanjani ngaphandle kokuziguqulela ezimangalweni zokusebenza ezingasekelwe.

Impendulo eqondile

Ukucaciswa kwekhabhoni ecushiwe okokufaka kokuqhathanisa, hhayi ukuqagela okuphelele kokusebenza kwenkambu. Igobolondo likakhukhunathi, amalahle kanye nokhuni kungakhiqiza izakhiwo ezihlukene zezimbotshana ngemva kokuqaliswa, kodwa amagama ezinto ezingavuthiwe ewodwa awasho ukufaneleka. Inombolo ye-iodine iyinani lezinhlamvu futhi akufanele yethulwe njengomthamo ongcolisayo wendawo yonke. I-Mesh ichaza ukusatshalaliswa kosayizi wezinhlayiyana; umlotha, umswakama kanye nobulukhuni bubhekana nezinye izinto ezibonakalayo. Ukugeza nge-asidi noma okunye ukwelapha ngemva kokwelashwa kufanele kuchazwe ngemininingwane enikeziwe kanye nobufakazi. Ukubuyekeza okunomthwalo wemfanelo kuxhumanisa ukucaciswa kwendawo okuhlanganisiwe okuhlosiwe, ikhemistri yamanzi, izimo zokuxhumana, ukwakhiwa kanye nohlelo lokulawula ikhwalithi ngaphambi kokwenza isincomo.

Qala ngenkinga yomthengi

Amashidi okuthenga ngokuvamile abeka inani lesihloko esisodwa eduze komthombo wekhabhoni futhi amema izinga elilula. Leso sinqamuleli singakha amaphutha amabili: singase senqabe into efanelekile ngenxa yokuthi inani elilodwa liphansi, noma ligunyaze into engafanele ngenxa yokuthi inani lingaphezulu. I-Adsorption incike ebudlelwaneni phakathi kwe-molecule eqondiwe, ukusatshalaliswa kwezimbotshana, ikhemistri engaphezulu, izinto eziqhudelanayo nezimo zokusebenza. Isitifiketi sokuhlaziya sichaza izakhiwo ezikhethiwe zesampula noma inkatho; ayilingisi uhlelo lokwelapha oluphelele.

Umthengi kufanele futhi ahlukanise idatha yenqwaba yemidiya ebufakazini obuqediwe be-cartridge. I-carbon block ihlanganisa ikhabhoni, i-binder kanye nesakhiwo sokukhiqiza. Ikhatriji eliyimbudumbudu lihlanganisa izikrini, amaphedi, izinto zegobolondo nokusabalalisa kokugeleza. Ngisho nalapho kusetshenziswa izinga elifanayo lekhabhoni, umkhiqizo oqediwe ungakha ukuthintana okuhlukile nokuziphatha kokucindezela. Ngakho-ke, ukucaciswa kudinga ukulandeleka kusukela kwabezindaba abagunyaziwe kuya ekuqedeni ukwakhiwa futhi, lapho kufanele, kuye ekuhlolweni okuqondene nokungcola.

Imuphi umbiko nedatha yokusebenza edingekayo

Thumela okuhlosiwe kokwelashwa kanye nombiko wamanzi kanye nencazelo ehlongozwayo. Ukubuyekeza kufanele kuhlonze ukuthi yimaphi amanani ayizilawuli zokuthengwa kwempahla, okuyizinto ezifakiwe zenqubo futhi adinga ukuhlolwa okuhlukile komkhiqizo oqediwe.

  • i-carbon eluhlaza kanye nencazelo yokwenza kusebenze noma ngemva kokwelashwa
  • inombolo ye-iodine enezindlela zokuhlola kanye nesisekelo sokubika
  • usayizi wezinhlayiyana noma ukusatshalaliswa kwemeshi futhi kuvunyelwe usayizi ongaphansi noma owedlulele
  • umlotha, umswakama kanye nobulukhuni ngezindlela zokuhlola
  • isimo sokugezwa nge-asidi noma okunye ukwelashwa ngamakhemikhali kanye nesimiso sakho sokwamukela
  • ukuminyana okusobala kanye nesisekelo sokupakisha lapho kuhlelwa ukulayisha umkhumbi
  • ukungcola okuhlosiwe, izakhi zamanzi eziqhudelanayo kanye nezimo zokusebenza
  • ukulandeleka okuningi, imvamisa yesampula kanye nezidingo zesitifiketi sokuhlaziya

Umbiko kufanele ube wakamuva ngokwanele ukuze umelele umthombo ohlongozwayo futhi kufanele uhlonze idethi yesampula nendawo. Uma amanzi eshintsha ngesizini, inqwaba yokukhiqiza noma isimo sokusebenza, hlinzeka ngobubanzi kunomphumela owodwa olula. Yisho inhloso yamanzi ahlanzekile adingekayo ngokwehlukana nokuhlaziywa kwamanje. Uma inani lingaziwa, limaka njengelingaziwa; igebe elibonakalayo liphephile kunenombolo eqageliwe.

Ulwazi lwe-hydraulic luyingxenye yekhemistri. Ukugeleza okumaphakathi kukodwa kungafihla izimo ezimfushane eziphakeme, umsebenzi wezikhawu kanye nokuma okude. Qinisekisa isikhathi sokusebenza sansuku zonke, isidingo esiphezulu, ingcindezi etholakalayo, ukulahlekelwa kwengcindezi okuvunyelwe, ubukhulu bemishini futhi WHO izoqapha futhi imiselele ikhabhoni. Le mininingwane inquma ukuthi imidiya ebambekayo ingasetshenziswa yini kusistimu esebenzisekayo.

I-logic yokukhetha

Qala ngomsebenzi wevelu ngayinye. Usayizi wezinhlayiyana unomthelela ekuziphatheni kwe-hydraulic kanye nendawo yangaphandle etholakalayo, kuyilapho izinhlawulo ezeqile zingathinta ukuphatha kanye nezisetshenziswa ezingezansi zomfula. Ukuqina kungahlotshaniswa nokuncipha ngesikhathi sokuthutha, ukulayisha noma ukuwashwa ngemuva. Umswakama uthinta izibalo zenqwaba ezitholwayo. Umlotha uchaza izinsalela ezingewona ikhabhoni ngaphansi kwendlela eshiwo. Le mingcele ingase isekele ukulawulwa kwekhwalithi, kodwa ngayinye kufanele ihunyushwe ngendlela yayo kanye nebanga lokwamukela.

Phatha inombolo ye-iodine njengomphumela wesici esisodwa esibalulekile, hhayi isithembiso esiqondile se-chlorine, PFAS, ama-VOC, umbala noma enye inkomba yokwelashwa. Amakhabhoni amabili anamanani afanayo e-iodine angase aziphathe ngendlela ehlukile kwinhlanganisela ethile ngenxa yokuthi ukusakazeka kwezimbotshana kanye nezindawo ezingaphezulu ziyehluka. Ngakolunye uhlangothi, umehluko wenombolo ye-iodine awuwubalanga ngokwawo umehluko empilweni yesevisi. Ubufakazi obuqondene nokungcola kanye nezimo zephrojekthi kusadingeka.

Izinto ezingavuthiwe kufanele ziqondise imibuzo kunokuqeda ukukhetha. Igobolondo likakhukhunathi, amalahle asuselwa kumalahle kanye namakhabhoni asenziwe ngokhuni akhiqizwa kusuka ezanduleli ezihlukene futhi angakhethelwa izimbotshana ezihlukene noma izinjongo zokuphatha, kodwa ukukhetha ukukhiqiza nokuvula kubalulekile. Cela ukucaciswa kwamanje komphakeli, izindlela zokuhlola, isitifiketi senkatho kanye nomkhuba wokulawula ushintsho. Uma abezindaba bezosekela umkhiqizo oqediwe oqinisekisiwe, qinisekisa ukuthi ukushintshwa kulawulwa imithetho esebenzayo yokufakwa kuhlu nezitifiketi.

Ukwelashwa kwangaphambili, okokusebenza kanye nesimo sokusebenza

Emikhumbini, ukucaciswa kufanele kuxhumeke kusisindo sokulayisha, umthamo wombhede, ukunwetshwa kwe-hydraulic, ukugcinwa kwesikrini, i-backwash nokuphathwa kwezinhlawulo. Kuma-cartridge, qinisekisa ukuthi ingabe inani lichaza imidiya exegayo ngaphambi kokukhiqizwa noma into eqediwe. I-block cartridge ayikwazi ukubonakala ngokugcwele ngesitifiketi sekhabhoni eyinqwaba, futhi iyunithi ye-granular esemgqeni isadinga ukwakhiwa, ubukhulu kanye nokuhlolwa kwengcindezi.

Ukuhlola okungenayo kufanele kusebenzise amasampula okuvunyelenwe ngawo nezindlela. Imiphumela yomphakeli nomthengi ingahluka uma isisekelo somswakama, ukulungiswa kwesampula noma izinguqulo zendlela zingaqondani. Ngakho-ke, ikhotheshini nerekhodi lokugunyazwa kufanele lisho uhlobo olucacisiwe futhi lichaze ukuthi izikatho ezingaphandle kwebanga ziphathwa kanjani. Yuchen Water ingasekela le mibhalo kanye nokumataniswa kwezinto ezisetshenziswayo ezigunyaziwe namakhathriji noma okokusebenza.

Ubufakazi kanye nomngcele wesevisi

Isitifiketi sokuhlaziya kufanele sethulwe njengobufakazi obuningi noma isampula. Akumele ibhalwe kabusha njengesiqinisekiso sempilo yesevisi, umthamo wesistimu noma ukugxiliswa kwe-outlet. Lapho i-Yuchen Water ishicilela idatha yokuhlola ekhethiwe, ikhasi kufanele lihlonze isampula ehloliwe kanye nemibandela futhi limeme umthengi ukuthi acele ubufakazi obufanayo kunokusikisela ukuthi yonke imikhiqizo yabelana ngomphumela.

Ukunikezwa kwezinto ezibonakalayo kungabandakanya ukucaciswa okubuyekeziwe, amadokhumenti okuhlola atholakalayo kanye nosekelo lokufanisa okokusebenza. Amanani okwamukela, ukuvama kokuhlolwa kanye nezimangalo zomkhiqizo oqediwe kufanele kuvunyelwane nge-oda langempela. Uma isimangalo esidingekayo singasekelwa ubufakazi bamanje, umphumela olungile wokubuyekezwa uyigebe lobufakazi noma ukuhlolwa okuhlongozwayo, hhayi ukucabangela kokumaketha.

Yuchen Water ibuyekeza okuhlosiwe kanye nobufakazi kuqala, bese ihlonza ifomethi yekhabhoni efanele kanye nemibuzo ebalulekile, esekela ukwelapha kwangaphambili nezidingo zemishini, kanye namadokhumenti noma izivivinyo ezisadingeka. Okukhiphayo kungaba isiqondiso sokuqala, isicelo solwazi olungekho, noma isincomo sento esekelayo nesisetshenziswa. Isincomo asikhishwa ngoba nje umthengi unikeze igama lomkhiqizo.

Ngemuva kokuhlinzekwa, ukwesekwa kwezobuchwepheshe kungafaka ukuqinisekiswa kwedokhumenti, imibuzo yokufaka kanye ne-flushing, isiqondiso sokusebenza esikude, izindawo zokuqapha kanye nokuhlelwa kokushintsha ngaphakathi kwendawo okuvunyelwene ngayo. Ukusebenza kwangempela kusancike ezintweni ezigunyaziwe, okokusebenza okuphelele, amanzi omthombo, ukufakwa, ukusebenza nokugcinwa. Isiqondiso esishicilelwe asikwazi ukufaka esikhundleni sokubuyekezwa kwephrojekthi.

Akukho lutho lokugcina, umthamo, impilo yesevisi, iphesenti lokususwa noma umphumela wephrojekthi othenjiswayo ngaphambi kokubuyekezwa kombiko, ukwakhiwa, ubufakazi kanye nezimo zokusebenza.

Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

Ingabe inombolo ephezulu ye-iodine ifakazela impilo ende yesevisi?

Cha. Inombolo ye-iodine iyinani lezinhlamvu. Impilo yesevisi incike kulokho okuhlosiwe, ikhemistri yamanzi, imidiya, izimo zesistimu nendawo yokugcina.

Ingabe ikhabhoni egezwe nge-asidi isusa ngokuzenzakalelayo ukungcola okwengeziwe?

Cha. Ukugeza nge-asidi kuyimininingwane yokwelapha. Imibandela yayo yokufaneleka kanye nokwamukela kufanele ihlanganiswe nesicelo esihlosiwe kanye nobufakazi.

Ingabe isitifiketi esisodwa singakhava yonke indawo yokukhiqiza?

Isitifiketi ngokuvamile sichaza isampula ekhonjiwe noma inkatho ngaphansi kwezindlela ezishiwo. Ukunikezwa okuqhubekayo kudinga amasampula okuvunyelwene ngawo, ukulandeleka kanye nokulawula ushintsho.

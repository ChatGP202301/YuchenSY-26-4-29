Izimiso ze-RO ne-NF Membrane: Ukubamba Usawoti, I-Flux, I-Recovery Nengcindezi Ye-Osmotic

Awukwazi ukwahlulela ukusebenza kwe-RO/NF nge-TDS noma ngephesenti elilodwa lokubamba usawoti. Bheka ndawonye ukubamba usawoti, ukudlula kukasawoti, ukugeleza kwe-permeate, i-flux, i-recovery, izinga lokushisa, ingcindezi, ukwakheka kwamanzi angenayo nezimo zokuhlola ezishiwo zomkhiqizo. Isilinganiso se-element eyodwa asiqinisekisi ikhwalithi ye-permeate yesistimu yonke.

Izincazelo ezisemqoka

Ukubamba usawoti okubonwayo kuvame ukubalwa ngokuthi (1 − ukugxila ku-permeate ÷ ukugxila emanzini angenayo) × 100%. Ukudlula kukasawoti kungu-100% kususwe ukubamba. I-flux ukugeleza kwe-permeate kuhlukaniswe ngendawo ye-membrane esebenzayo. I-recovery ukugeleza kwe-permeate kuhlukaniswe ukugeleza kokuphakelayo. Bhala indawo yesampula, indlela yokulinganisa ukugxila, izimo zokusebenza nokuthi amanani enziwe i-normalization yini.

Esixazululweni esincibilike kancane esifanele imodeli ye-ideal, isilinganiso sokufundisa sengcindezi ye-osmotic sithi π = RTΣCi. Amayunithi kufanele ahambisane futhi indlela yokubala izinhlobo zezinto ezincibilikile icace. Isibonelo somphakeli sisebenzisa i-NaCl engu-2 g/L ku-25°C: C≈0.0342 mol/L futhi π≈169 kPa (1.69 bar). Lesi yisilinganiso kuphela, asiyona imodeli ephelele ye-seawater activity.

Ithebula lomthombo labhala ngokungalungile i-NaCl 34.2 ne-MgSO₄ 16.7 njenge-mol/L. Uma kusetshenziswa ama-molecular weight alo kanye nokufakwa kokuqala okungu-2,000 mg/L, amayunithi afanele yi-mmol/L (0.0342 no-0.0167 mol/L). Lokhu kulungiswa kushiwo ngokucacile; ungaphindi iyunithi eyaphrintwa ngephutha.

Kungani izimo zokusebenza zibalulekile

Ingcindezi esebenzayo ephezulu ingakhuphula i-water flux; izinga lokushisa lishintsha i-viscosity nokuthuthwa kukasawoti; usawoti omningi emanzini angenayo ukhuphula ingcindezi ye-osmotic; i-pH nokwakheka kwama-ion kungathinta i-membrane nokwakheka kwe-scale. Ngenxa ye-concentration polarization, ukugxila ebusweni be-membrane kungadlula okusemanzini amakhulu, ngakho i-apparent rejection ne-intrinsic rejection ziyehluka. Umphumela uncike ohlotsheni lwe-membrane, ekuhlelweni kwe-element, ku-recovery nasendleleni yokuhlola.

Isibonelo somphakeli sinikeza i-LP22-8040 ene-stable rejection engu-99.7%, ukukhiqiza okungu-10,500 GPD nendawo engu-400 ft²; ngokuhlukanisa kutholakala 26.25 GFD. Lezi yizilinganiso zale modeli kuphela futhi kufanele ziqhathaniswe ngaphansi kwezimo zokuhlola ezishiwo. Isilinganiso se-element asibikezeli ikhwalithi ye-permeate yesistimu yonke futhi asiqinisekisi ukusebenza kwesitshalo.

Okufanele kuthunyelwe ukuze kubuyekezwe

Thumela ukuhlaziywa kwamanzi okuphelele kwakamuva, ikhwalithi yamanzi angenayo ne-permeate ehlosiwe, ukugeleza okudingekayo, ibanga lokushisa, i-pH, i-recovery ehlosiwe, ingcindezi yokusebenza, i-pretreatment, izigaba kanye nokusebenza kwamanje okwenziwe i-normalization uma ushintsha ama-element. I-TDS iyodwa ayibonisi ama-ion abangela i-scale, i-silica, ama-organic, ama-oxidant noma ubungozi be-fouling.

Imikhawulo nesinyathelo esilandelayo

Lo mhlahlandlela wokufundisa usekelwe emininingwaneni yobuchwepheshe bomphakeli nasezimisweni ezijwayelekile zama-membrane. Awusona isiqinisekiso somklamo noma umyalelo wokusebenzisa. Ukukhetha kokugcina kudinga ukuqinisekiswa kwemibhalo yemodeli ngqo, ukwakheka kwamanzi okuphelele nezimo zendawo. I-Yuchen Water ingasiza ukubuyekeza ulwazi lwe-membrane/material nesistimu nokukhomba okushodayo. Cela ukubuyekezwa kokuhlaziywa kwamanzi ngemininingwane engenhla.

Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

Ingabe u-99.7% wokubamba kukasawoti kwe-element usho ukuthi i-permeate yesitshalo izoba ngu-99.7%?

Cha. Umphumela uncike ekwakhekeni kwamanzi angenayo, ezimweni zokusebenza, eminyakeni ye-element, ezigabeni, kuma-seal, ekuxubeni nasendleleni yokukala.

Ingabe i-TDS yodwa inganquma ukuthi i-RO noma i-NF iyafaneleka?

Cha. Ukwakheka kwama-ion, ikhwalithi ehlosiwe, i-recovery, i-scaling nemikhawulo yenqubo kubalulekile.

Ingabe i-flux iyafana namandla okukhiqiza i-permeate?

Cha. I-flux ilinganiswa ngendawo ye-membrane; amandla okukhiqiza ancike nasendaweni, ezimweni zokusebenza nasekuhlelweni kwesistimu.

Ukuklama Isistimu ye-RO Yezimboni: I-Recovery, I-Concentration Factor Nezigaba

I-RO recovery ukugeleza kwe-permeate kuhlukaniswe ukugeleza kwamanzi angenayo. Ukuyiphakamisa kunciphisa ukugeleza kwe-concentrate kodwa kukhuphula usawoti ku-concentrate, futhi kungandisa i-scaling, concentration polarization nobungozi bokusebenza. I-recovery yomklamo kufanele ibalwe kusetshenziswa ukuhlaziywa kwamanzi okuphelele, ukugeleza okuhlosiwe, imodeli ye-membrane, izinga lokushisa nemikhawulo yesistimu enezigaba—hhayi umthetho ojwayelekile.

I-Mass balance ne-concentration factor

Esimweni sokugeleza esizinzile: amanzi angenayo = permeate + concentrate. Uma kunganakwa i-salt passage futhi usawoti ungawi njenge-precipitate, i-concentration factor elinganisiwe ithi 1/(1 − recovery): 75% → 4×, 80% → 5×, 90% → 10×. Lezi yizibonelo zezibalo kuphela, aziyona imiphumela yomklamo. Isistimu yangempela idinga ukuhlolwa kwe-salt passage, precipitation, antiscalant, izinga lokushisa, izigaba nemikhawulo ye-recovery.

Isibonelo sokusebenza somphakeli sithi amanzi angenayo angu-80 m³/h, concentrate engu-20 m³/h ne-permeate engu-60 m³/h, ngakho i-recovery ingu-75%. Siphinde silinganise i-recovery nge-TDS yamanzi angenayo/permeate/concentrate. Le mass balance iyisheke lokuxilonga kuphela uma amasampula emele, isitshalo sizinzile, amathuluzi e-calibrated futhi izilinganiso zokugxila ziqhathaniseka. I-TDS ingase ingalandeli ngokunembile i-ion ngayinye ebangela i-scaling.

Umkhawulo we-design case

Icala lesofthiwe yokuklama amanzi angaphansi komhlaba emininingwaneni yobuchwepheshe enikeziwe (amakhasi omthombo 33–45) libika i-permeate engu-100 m³/h, 25°C ne-recovery engu-75%; liphinde likhulume nge-flux engu-27 LMH, cishe ama-element angu-100 kanye ne-interstage boost engu-2.5 bar esibonelweni sokuthuthukisa. Ukuhlaziywa kwamanzi okuphelele, amafayela okufaka/okukhipha esofthiwe nokuhlelwa kwe-membrane akutholakali ukuze kuphindwe ngokuzimela. Lezi zinombolo ziyisimo sesibonelo somphakeli kuphela, akuwona umklamo onconywayo noma indawo yokusebenza eqinisekisiwe.

Okufanele unjiniyela akuthole

Kudingeka ukuhlaziywa okuphelele kwama-ion (kuhlanganise nobulukhuni, i-alkalinity, i-sulfate, i-silica, i-barium/strontium, i-iron nama-organic lapho kufanele), ukuguquguquka kwamanzi angenayo, ukwakheka ne-flow ye-permeate okuhlosiwe, izinga lokushisa, i-pH, i-pretreatment, i-recovery, imodeli ye-element, ukuhlelwa kwe-pressure vessel, imikhawulo yamandla, imikhawulo yokulahla i-concentrate nezindinganiso ezifanele. Umphumela owodwa we-TDS awanele.

Lokho i-Yuchen engakubuyekeza

I-Yuchen Water ingasiza ukubuyekeza ulwazi lwe-membrane nesistimu, ithole okufunwa ukuhlaziywa kwamanzi okushodayo futhi ixoxe ngezinketho zama-membrane/material ezihambisanayo. Umklami wesistimu oqeqeshiwe unesibopho somklamo wokugcina futhi kufanele awuqhathanise nemanuwali yemodeli yamanje nezimo zendawo. Thumela ukuhlaziywa, ikhwalithi yamanzi omkhiqizo ehlosiwe, ukugeleza, izinga lokushisa, i-pH, i-recovery, i-pretreatment nokuhlelwa kwamanje ukuze kubuyekezwe ngaphansi kobubanzi obucacile.

Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

Ingabe i-recovery ephezulu ihlale yehlisa izindleko?

Cha. Inganciphisa ivolumu ye-concentrate kodwa inyuse izindleko zengcindezi, zokulawula i-scaling, zokuhlanza noma zokwethembeka.

Ingabe i-recovery engu-75% yesibonelo ingasetshenziswa kwenye indawo?

Cha. Ukwakheka kwamanzi angenayo nemikhawulo yesistimu kunquma i-recovery evumelekile.

Ingabe i-TDS ingabikezela i-scaling?

Cha. Kudingeka ukugxila kwe-ion ngayinye, i-pH, izinga lokushisa nokugxila ku-concentrate.

I-Industrial GAC Idizayini: EBCT, Ukuphumelela kanye Nokuqapha

Bona ukuthi ukugeleza, umthamo wombhede, EBCT, ukulahleka kwengcindezi, ukuqapha okudlulele, ukumiswa komkhumbi kanye nokuhlelwa kwesevisi kuwubumba kanjani iphrojekthi yemboni yembudumbudu yekhabhoni ecushiwe.

Impendulo eqondile

Uhlelo lwezimboni GAC luklanywe ngokuzungeza impokophelo yokwelashwa kanye nesu lokulawula impumelelo, hhayi mayelana nenqwaba yemidiya yodwa. Isikhathi sokuxhumana sombhede ongenalutho, noma EBCT, sihlobanisa umthamo wombhede ongenalutho ukuze ugeleze, kodwa kuwukufaka okukodwa komklamo. Ukujula kombhede, izinga lokulayisha, ikhemikhali yamanzi, izinto eziphilayo eziqhudelanayo, izinga lokushisa, ukulawulwa kwezinhlayiyana, ukusatshalaliswa kokugeleza kanye nekhabhoni ekhethiwe konke kuthinta ukusebenza. Idizayini nayo idinga imikhawulo yengcindezi, amasampula amasampula, i-outlet trigger echaziwe, i-backwash noma izinhlinzeko zokuphatha izinhlawulo, kanye nohlelo lokushintsha noma lokuvuselela. Yuchen Water ingahlinzeka ngezinto zekhabhoni ecushiwe futhi inikeze usekelo lwezobuchwepheshe lwemishini ngemva kokubuyekezwa kombiko, umsebenzi we-hydraulic kanye nezimfuneko zokuqapha.

Qala ngenkinga yomthengi

Umthengi angase akwazi ukugeleza okudingekayo kodwa hhayi indawo yokulayisha engcolile noma indawo yokugcina eqondiwe. Kuleso simo, ukulinganiswa komkhumbi akukwazi ukusungula impilo yemidiya. Enye iphrojekthi ingaba nekhemistri enemininingwane kodwa ingenalo ulwazi mayelana nokugeleza okuphezulu, umjikelezo womsebenzi noma ingcindezi etholakalayo. Zombili izikhala zinendaba. Umbhalo omfishane wezobuchwepheshe kufanele uhlanganise ikhwalithi yamanzi, ama-hydraulic kanye nokusebenza ukuze uhlelo lungalinganiswe ngesimo esijwayelekile esinyamalalayo phakathi nesidingo esikhulu noma ushintsho lwesizini.

Ukuphumelela akuhambisani nokukhathala okuphelele. Kuyiphuzu lapho inhlanganisela egadwayo noma omunye umuntu ongene esikhundleni afinyelela khona kusibangeli esichazwe yiphrojekthi. Leso sibangeli singasethwa ngaphambi kokuthobelana noma umkhawulo wenqubo ukuze kuhlinzekwe imajini yokusebenza. Izinhlanganisela ezihlukene zingabhodloza ngezikhathi ezihlukene, futhi izinto eziphilayo zemvelo zingathatha umthamo we-adsorption. Iphrojekthi kufanele isho ukuthi yini ezokalwa, lapho amasampula azothathwa khona, azohlaziywa kangaki nokuthi yisiphi isenzo esilandela umphumela.

Imuphi umbiko nedatha yokusebenza edingekayo

Ukubuyekezwa kwemboni kudinga iphrofayili yekhemistri emele kanye nemvilophu yokusebenza ye-hydraulic. Umphumela owodwa we-TDS awukwazi ukuchaza umsebenzi we-adsorption noma ukuziphatha komkhumbi.

  • inhlanganisela eqondiwe, ububanzi bokungena, okuqondiwe kokuphumayo kanye nendlela yokuhlaziya
  • TOC noma COD, izinkomba zezinto eziphilayo zemvelo nezinhlanganisela eziqhudelanayo
  • i-pH, izinga lokushisa, i-turbidity, izinto eziqinile ezimisiwe kanye nomongo webhayoloji
  • ukugeleza okumaphakathi, ubuncane kanye nokuphezulu kanye nesikhathi sokusebenza sansuku zonke nokuhlukahluka kokugeleza
  • ukucindezela okukhona, ukulahlekelwa kwengcindezi okuvumelekile kanye nezidingo ezingezansi zomfula
  • Ububanzi bomkhumbi obuhleliwe, ukujula kombhede, umthamo wombhede kanye nomqondo wokusabalalisa uma kwaziwa
  • izindawo zamasampula, imvamisa, isiqalisi sempumelelo nesikhathi sokuphendula
  • ukushintsha kwemidiya, ukwenziwa kabusha, ukuwasha ngemuva, ukudonsa amanzi kanye nohlelo lwemidiya esetshenzisiwe

Umbiko kufanele ube wakamuva ngokwanele ukuze umelele umthombo ohlongozwayo futhi kufanele uhlonze idethi yesampula nendawo. Uma amanzi eshintsha ngesizini, inqwaba yokukhiqiza noma isimo sokusebenza, hlinzeka ngobubanzi kunomphumela owodwa olula. Yisho inhloso yamanzi ahlanzekile adingekayo ngokwehlukana nokuhlaziywa kwamanje. Uma inani lingaziwa, limaka njengelingaziwa; igebe elibonakalayo liphephile kunenombolo eqageliwe.

Ulwazi lwe-hydraulic luyingxenye yekhemistri. Ukugeleza okumaphakathi kukodwa kungafihla izimo ezimfushane eziphakeme, umsebenzi wezikhawu kanye nokuma okude. Qinisekisa isikhathi sokusebenza sansuku zonke, isidingo esiphezulu, ingcindezi etholakalayo, ukulahlekelwa kwengcindezi okuvunyelwe, ubukhulu bemishini futhi WHO izoqapha futhi imiselele ikhabhoni. Le mininingwane inquma ukuthi imidiya ebambekayo ingasetshenziswa yini kusistimu esebenzisekayo.

I-logic yokukhetha

Bala EBCT kumthamo wombhede ongenalutho ohlongozwayo kanye nokugeleza kokusebenza, bese uhlola ukuthi ububanzi obugcwele behydraulic bugcina isisekelo somklamo ohlosiwe. Ungashicileli eyodwa EBCT njengemfuneko yendawo yonke yakho konke ukungcola. Inani kufanele lisekelwe ubufakazi obufanele, umsebenzi wokuhlola, isiqondiso sabahlinzeki noma idizayini yenqubo efanelekayo. Ukugeleza okuphezulu, ukusebenza okuhambisanayo kanye nemikhumbi engasebenzi ingashintsha ngokubonakalayo ukuxhumana okukhona.

Buyekeza ukusatshalaliswa kokugeleza ngokulandelayo. Abasabalalisi abampofu, ukulayisha okungalingani, umoya ovalelwe noma ukuphazamiseka kombhede kungakha izindlela ezikhethwayo ezifinyeza ukuxhumana okusebenzayo. Ukujula kombhede kanye nejiyomethri yomkhumbi kufanele kusebenze nosayizi wezinhlayiyana ezikhethiwe, izikrini zokugcina kanye namasu okugeza i-backwash. Amageji wokucindezela noma ama-transmitter kuwo wonke umkhumbi ahlinzeka ngesignali yokusebenza, kodwa ukulahlekelwa kwengcindezi kukodwa akuvezi impumelelo ye-adsorption.

Khetha ukuqapha nokulandelana komkhumbi ndawonye. Imikhumbi ye-lead-lag inganikeza umgoqo wokupholisha futhi ivumele izikhundla zokusebenza ukuba zijikelezwe ngemva kokuba umbhede oholayo ufinyelele i-trigger yawo. Imikhumbi ehambisanayo ingase inikeze amandla okugeleza kodwa idinga ukusatshalaliswa okulinganayo kanye nesu lokusampula isitimela ngasinye. Ukuhlelwa kufanele kubonise izidingo zokuqhubeka, ukuguqulwa kohlaziyo, ukufinyelela kwesevisi yabezindaba kanye nomphumela wokweqa okuqondiwe kokuphumayo.

Ukwelashwa kwangaphambili, okokusebenza kanye nesimo sokusebenza

Ukwelashwa kwangaphambili kuvikela ukusebenza kwe-hydraulic kanye ne-adsorption. Ukulawula okuqinile kunganciphisa ukonakala kanye nokwakheka kwesiteshi, kuyilapho ikhemistri ekhuphuka nomfula ingase idinge ukulungiswa ngenqubo eqondiwe. Uma ama-oxidants noma ukukhula kwebhayoloji kufanelekile, umklamo kufanele ubhekane nakho ngokucacile. Izinga lamanzi okugeza ngemuva, ukugeleza nokulahlwa nakho kudinga ukubuyekezwa; ukunikezwa kwe-backwash akunalo inani uma isayithi lingakwazi ukuhlinzeka ngesimo esidingekayo se-hydraulic.

Ukugunyazwa kufanele kubhale phansi ubunikazi bemidiya, inani elilayishiwe, ukujula kombhede, ukuguquguquka kokuqala, ukucindezela okuyisisekelo kanye nemiphumela yokuhlaziya yokuqala. Ukusebenza kufanele kuthambekele ukugeleza, ingcindezi, imiphumela yamasampula kanye nemicimbi yesevisi. Ukuhlelwa kokufaka esikhundleni kufanele kubandakanye ukutholakala kwemidiya, ukuhlukaniswa kwemikhumbi, ukuphatha okuphephile nokuqinisekisa ngemva kokubuyela enkonzweni. Yuchen Water ingasekela imibuzo ebalulekile yamadokhumenti, ukuhlinzeka kanye nempahla ngaphakathi kwalo mkhakha ochaziwe.

Amaphutha ajwayelekile

  • Ukuphatha EBCT njengokuwukuphela komthetho wokulinganisa
  • Ukubikezela impilo yemidiya kusuka kumakhilogremu ekhabhoni ngaphandle kokulayisha okungcolile
  • Ukusebenzisa ukwehla kwengcindezi esikhundleni sokuhlolwa kwempumelelo
  • Iyeka ukugeleza okuphezulu, ukuphuma komkhumbi kanye nokuguqulwa kokuhlaziya kusukela kumklamo
  • Akuhlinzeki ngendawo yesampula noma ileveli yesenzo ngaphambi komkhawulo wokukhipha

Ubufakazi kanye nomngcele wesevisi

Umqondo womkhumbi wokuqala ungakhomba izikhala zolwazi kanye nezinhlelo ezingaba khona, kodwa awuwona umklamo wokusebenza oqinisekisiwe. Ukuqagela kwamandla kudinga ubufakazi obuhambisana nesicelo kanye nokuqagela okubonakala kumthengi. Lapho ukungaqiniseki kuyinto ephathekayo, ibhentshi noma ukuhlolwa komshayeli kanye nohlelo lokuqapha olulandelanayo kungase kube okufanelekile ngaphambi kokuzibophezela kokugcina kwezohwebo.

Yuchen Water indima ka-Yuchen Water ukuhlinzekwa kwempahla yekhabhoni ecushiwe kanye nomkhumbi ohlobene, ukuhlunga kanye nokwesekwa kobuchwepheshe kwemishini. Ubunjiniyela besayithi, ukuthobela imithetho yendawo, ukuphepha kwenqubo kanye nokwamukelwa kokugcina kokusebenza kuhlala kuqondene nephrojekthi ethile. Alikho ikhasi elishicilelwe okufanele lithembise EBCT, impilo yokulala, amaphesenti okususwa noma isikhawu sokushintsha ngaphandle kwesisekelo esigunyaziwe, esibhaliwe.

Yuchen Water ibuyekeza okuhlosiwe kanye nobufakazi kuqala, bese ihlonza ifomethi yekhabhoni efanele kanye nemibuzo ebalulekile, esekela ukwelapha kwangaphambili nezidingo zemishini, kanye namadokhumenti noma izivivinyo ezisadingeka. Okukhiphayo kungaba isiqondiso sokuqala, isicelo solwazi olungekho, noma isincomo sento esekelayo nesisetshenziswa. Isincomo asikhishwa ngoba nje umthengi unikeze igama lomkhiqizo.

Ngemuva kokuhlinzekwa, ukwesekwa kwezobuchwepheshe kungafaka ukuqinisekiswa kwedokhumenti, imibuzo yokufaka kanye ne-flushing, isiqondiso sokusebenza esikude, izindawo zokuqapha kanye nokuhlelwa kokushintsha ngaphakathi kwendawo okuvunyelwene ngayo. Ukusebenza kwangempela kusancike ezintweni ezigunyaziwe, okokusebenza okuphelele, amanzi omthombo, ukufakwa, ukusebenza nokugcinwa. Isiqondiso esishicilelwe asikwazi ukufaka esikhundleni sokubuyekezwa kwephrojekthi.

Akukho lutho lokugcina, umthamo, impilo yesevisi, iphesenti lokususwa noma umphumela wephrojekthi othenjiswayo ngaphambi kokubuyekezwa kombiko, ukwakhiwa, ubufakazi kanye nezimo zokusebenza.

Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

Ingabe i-EBCT iyafana nesikhathi sokuxhumana sangempela?

EBCT iwubudlelwane bomklamo osuselwe kumthamo wombhede ongenalutho nokugeleza. Ukuziphatha kwangempela kwe-hydraulic kungathinteka yimidiya, ukusatshalaliswa, i-voids kanye namashaneli.

Ingabe ukulahlekelwa kwengcindezi kungabonisa lapho ikhabhoni isiphelile?

Cha. Ukulahlekelwa kwengcindezi kuyinkomba ye-hydraulic. Ukuphumelela kudinga ukuqapha kwenhlanganisela eqondiwe noma umuntu oshade naye ogunyaziwe.

Kungani kufanele usebenzise imikhumbi yokuhola?

Ukuhlelwa kwe-lead-lag kunganikeza umgoqo wokupholisha kanye nokuguquguquka kokusebenza, kodwa ukufaneleka kwawo kuncike ekugelezeni, ukuqapha, ukuqhubeka kanye nezidingo zesevisi.

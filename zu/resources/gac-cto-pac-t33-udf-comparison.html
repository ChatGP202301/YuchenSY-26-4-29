GAC vs CTO vs PAC vs UDF vs T33: Ahluke Kanjani Amafomethi Ekhabhoni

Qhathanisa amafomethi ekhabhoni ecushiwe ngendima yokufaka, iphethini yokuxhumana, ukuziphatha kwengcindezi, indlela yesevisi kanye nezimfuneko zobufakazi esikhundleni sokuphatha amagama azo njenganokushintshwa.

Impendulo eqondile

GAC, CTO, PAC, UDF kanye ne-T33 bachaza amafomu emidiya ahlukene, ukwakhiwa kwekhatriji noma izikhundla zesistimu. GAC i-granular activated carbon esetshenziswa ezitsheni noma kuma-cartridges. CTO ivamise ukusho i-carbon-block cartridge, kuyilapho i-UDF ivame ukusetshenziselwa i-granular-carbon cartridge. T33 ngokuvamile ichaza i-post-carbon polishing cartridge. PAC iyimpuphu ikhabhoni eyenziwe yasebenza idlulwe emanzini futhi ikhishwe esinyathelweni sokuhlukanisa kamuva. Lawa malebula awafakazisi isimangalo sokususwa noma enze amafomethi ashintshashintshane. Ukukhethwa kufanele kuxhumanise umbiko wamanzi kanye nenhlanganisela eqondiwe nezimo zokuthintana, ukugeleza, ukulahlekelwa kwengcindezi, ukulawulwa kwezinhlayiyana, ubukhulu bemishini, ukufinyelela kwesevisi kanye nobufakazi bamanje bemidiya ehlongozwayo nokwakhiwa.

Qala ngenkinga yomthengi

Umthengi we-OEM angase adinge i-cartridge yokubuyisela ehlangene, isiteji sokupholisha ukunambitheka okusemgqeni noma imidiya eyinqwaba yomkhumbi omkhulu wokwelapha. Umklami wezimboni angase aqhathanise umbhede GAC ongashintshi PAC inqubo yokudosa. Amagama angabukeka njengeladi lomkhiqizo, kodwa achaza izindlela ezahlukene zokuhlanganisa amanzi nekhabhoni. Ngakho-ke isiqhathaniso esiwusizo siyasebenza: lapho i-carbon ihlala khona, indlela amanzi ahamba ngayo noma ayizungezile, ukuthi kungakanani ukuthintana okukhona, nokuthi ikhabhoni ephelile itholwa futhi ishintshwe.

Imininingwane yokuxhumana neyokwakha ibalulekile njengegama lemidiya. I-block cartridge ingahlanganisa i-adsorption nokuziphatha kokuhlunga ngokomzimba futhi ingase idale iphrofayili yengcindezi ehlukile kusuka kumbhede oyimbudumbudu oxekethile. I-granular cartridge idinga izikrini noma amaphedi agcina imidiya futhi alawule izinhlawulo. Iyunithi ye-inline T33 kufanele ifane nokufakwa, umumo nomsebenzi wokugcina wokupholisha. PAC idinga ukudoswa, ukuxutshwa, ukuthintana kanye nokuhlukaniswa kwezinto eziqinile ezansi nomfula; ayifakiwe njenge-cartridge evamile yokugeleza.

Imuphi umbiko nedatha yokusebenza edingekayo

Chaza uhlelo lokusebenza ngaphambi kokuqamba ifomethi. Ulwazi olulandelayo luvumela umphakeli ukuthi anqume ukuthi ngabe iphrojekthi idinga indawo yomkhumbi, i-cartridge eshintshwayo, iyunithi yokupholisha emgqeni noma inqubo ehlukile yokudosa.

  • umthombo wamanzi kanye nokuhlaziywa okuphelele okutholakalayo
  • okuhlanganisiwe okuqondiwe noma indawo yokugcina yezinzwa kanye nomgomo wokuphuma odingekayo
  • ukugeleza okumaphakathi nokuphakeme, isikhathi sokusebenza sansuku zonke kanye nokusebenza kwezikhawu noma okuqhubekayo
  • izindlu ezitholakalayo, umkhumbi, uxhumano kanye nobukhulu bekhatriji
  • ukwehla kwengcindezi ehlanzekile kanye netheminali okuvumelekile
  • i-turbidity, okuqinile okumisiwe, TOC noma COD kanye nengqikithi yebhayoloji
  • izigaba zokwelashwa ezikhuphuka nomfula
  • ukufinyelela esikhundleni, indlela yokuphatha imfucuza kanye nohlelo lokuqapha

Umbiko kufanele ube wakamuva ngokwanele ukuze umelele umthombo ohlongozwayo futhi kufanele uhlonze idethi yesampula nendawo. Uma amanzi eshintsha ngesizini, inqwaba yokukhiqiza noma isimo sokusebenza, hlinzeka ngobubanzi kunomphumela owodwa olula. Yisho inhloso yamanzi ahlanzekile adingekayo ngokwehlukana nokuhlaziywa kwamanje. Uma inani lingaziwa, limaka njengelingaziwa; igebe elibonakalayo liphephile kunenombolo eqageliwe.

Ulwazi lwe-hydraulic luyingxenye yekhemistri. Ukugeleza okumaphakathi kukodwa kungafihla izimo ezimfushane eziphakeme, umsebenzi wezikhawu kanye nokuma okude. Qinisekisa isikhathi sokusebenza sansuku zonke, isidingo esiphezulu, ingcindezi etholakalayo, ukulahlekelwa kwengcindezi okuvunyelwe, ubukhulu bemishini futhi WHO izoqapha futhi imiselele ikhabhoni. Le mininingwane inquma ukuthi imidiya ebambekayo ingasetshenziswa yini kusistimu esebenzisekayo.

I-logic yokukhetha

Sebenzisa GAC engxoxweni yomkhumbi lapho iphrojekthi ingahlinzeka ngombhede ochaziwe, abasabalalisi, ukulayishwa kwe-hydraulic, amasampula kanye nensizakalo yemidiya. Sebenzisa ingxoxo ye-granular cartridge lapho ifomu lemidiya elifanayo kufanele lilingane nendlu eshintshwayo ngesilinganiso esincane kakhulu. Lezi zinhlelo zokusebenza azikwazi ukuqhathaniswa nenqwaba yemidiya iyodwa ngoba i-geometry yombhede, ukusatshalaliswa kokugeleza, ubungozi be-bypass kanye nesikhathi sokuxhumana siyahluka.

Sebenzisa CTO noma esinye isakhiwo se-carbon-block uma ifomethi ye-cartridge edingekayo, ukuziphatha kokugcinwa kwezinhlayiyana kanye nephrofayili yokucindezela kuhambisana nesistimu. Ukuminyana kwebhulokhi, isistimu yokuhlanganisa, ubukhulu kanye nokuvumelana kokukhiqiza kuba yingxenye yokubuyekezwa. Ilebula yokuvimba ayisunguli ukunciphisa ukungcola; ukwakhiwa okuhloliwe kanye nemibandela kufanele isekele noma yisiphi isimangalo somphakathi esithile.

Sebenzisa T33 njengencazelo yokuma kwesistimu kuphela ngemva kokuqinisekisa ukuthi iyunithi esemgqeni okulindeleke ukuthi ipholishwe nokuthi yini engena kuyo. Sebenzisa PAC lapho inqubo ihlanganisa umthamo olawulwayo kanye nokuhlukaniswa kwakamuva. PAC ukusebenza kuncike kumthamo, ukuxubana, ukuthintana, izinto eziqhudelanayo kanye nokususwa kwempushana esetshenzisiwe. Akumele ifakwe esikhundleni sokugeleza ngokudlula ngoba nje iphinde ibe yikhabhoni ecushiwe.

Ukwelashwa kwangaphambili, okokusebenza kanye nesimo sokusebenza

Ukuqhathanisa kufanele kufake ingcindezi ephelele kanye nendlela yokulungisa. Amasistimu e-Cartridge adinga ubukhulu obuqinisekisiwe, izindawo zokuvala, umumo nokufinyelela kokushintsha. Amasistimu emikhumbi adinga ukubuyekezwa komsabalalisi kanye ne-underdrain, isibonelelo sokunweba imibhede lapho kusebenza khona ukuwashwa, izindawo zokusampula kanye nohlelo lwemidiya esetshenzisiwe. PAC amasistimu adinga ukugcinwa okuphephile, okokusebenza kwemithamo, ukuhlakazwa kanye nomgoqo wokukhipha okuqinile.

Ukwelashwa kusengaphambili kungashintsha isinqumo. Izinto eziqinile eziphakeme zingavimba ama-cartridges noma zidle umthamo wombhede; izinto eziphilayo zemvelo zingaqhudelana nalokho okuhlosiwe; ama-oxidants nezimo zebhayoloji zithinta isu lesevisi. Ifomethi ikhethwa ngemva kokuba lezi zingozi sezichazwe, azisetshenziselwa ukufihla idatha yamanzi engekho. Yuchen Water angakwazi ukufanisa izinketho zokuhlinzeka kanye nezindlu ezihlobene noma usekelo lwemishini nomsebenzi obuyekeziwe.

Amaphutha ajwayelekile

  • Ukusebenzisa i-GAC kanye ne-UDF njengokungathi bahlala behlonza i-carbon chemistry ehlukile
  • Ukuphatha CTO njengobufakazi be-chlorine ethile noma isimangalo sokungcola
  • Kushayelwa yonke i-cartridge ye-carbon cartridge T33 ngaphandle kokuhlola umsebenzi nokuxhumana
  • Ifaka PAC ngenqubo ngaphandle kwesinyathelo sokuhlukaniswa komfula
  • Ukuqhathanisa amafomethi ngenani lokuthenga kuyilapho uziba ingcindezi, isevisi nokuqapha

Ubufakazi kanye nomngcele wesevisi

Ukuqhathaniswa kwefomethi akusona isitatimende sesitifiketi. Isitifiketi singesomkhiqizo osohlwini kanye nesimangalo ngaphansi kohlelo olufanele, akuzona zonke izinto ezabelana ngefomethi evamile. Ngokunjalo, umphumela wokuhlolwa ungowemithombo yezindaba ehloliwe nokwakhiwa. Ikhophi yasesidlangalaleni kufanele ilondoloze lobo budlelwano futhi igweme ukudlulisa umphumela phakathi kwemikhiqizo embudumbudu, ibhulokhi kanye ne-inline.

Yuchen Water ingahlinzeka ngokwakhiwa kwemidiya nekhathriji futhi isekele isibuyekezo sokufanisa imishini. Ifomethi yokugcina, ukucaciswa, inani kanye nohlelo lokushintshwa kuqinisekiswa kuphela ngemuva kokubuyekezwa kombiko wamanzi, ubukhulu, umsebenzi we-hydraulic kanye nenjongo yokwelashwa. Uma umthengi esachaza okokusebenza, okukhiphayo kokuqala kufanele kube isiqondiso sobuchwepheshe kanye nohlu lwezinto ezifakiwe ezingekho esikhundleni sokukhethwa kwezinto ngaphambi kwesikhathi.

Yuchen Water ibuyekeza okuhlosiwe kanye nobufakazi kuqala, bese ihlonza ifomethi yekhabhoni efanele kanye nemibuzo ebalulekile, esekela ukwelapha kwangaphambili nezidingo zemishini, kanye namadokhumenti noma izivivinyo ezisadingeka. Okukhiphayo kungaba isiqondiso sokuqala, isicelo solwazi olungekho, noma isincomo sento esekelayo nesisetshenziswa. Isincomo asikhishwa ngoba nje umthengi unikeze igama lomkhiqizo.

Ngemuva kokuhlinzekwa, ukwesekwa kwezobuchwepheshe kungafaka ukuqinisekiswa kwedokhumenti, imibuzo yokufaka kanye ne-flushing, isiqondiso sokusebenza esikude, izindawo zokuqapha kanye nokuhlelwa kokushintsha ngaphakathi kwendawo okuvunyelwene ngayo. Ukusebenza kwangempela kusancike ezintweni ezigunyaziwe, okokusebenza okuphelele, amanzi omthombo, ukufakwa, ukusebenza nokugcinwa. Isiqondiso esishicilelwe asikwazi ukufaka esikhundleni sokubuyekezwa kwephrojekthi.

Akukho lutho lokugcina, umthamo, impilo yesevisi, iphesenti lokususwa noma umphumela wephrojekthi othenjiswayo ngaphambi kokubuyekezwa kombiko, ukwakhiwa, ubufakazi kanye nezimo zokusebenza.

Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

Ingabe i-UDF iwumsebenzi we-adsorption ohlukile ku-GAC?

Akunjalo. UDF ngokuvamile ilebula i-granular activated-carbon cartridge. Qinisekisa umthombo wangempela wekhabhoni, ukwelashwa, ukwakhiwa kanye nencazelo enikeziwe.

Ingabe PAC ingapakishwa endlini yokuhlunga evamile?

PAC ngokuvamile kuxoxwa ngakho njengomthamo olandelwa ukuhlukaniswa, hhayi esikhundleni se-granular egciniwe noma i-block cartridge.

Iyiphi ifomethi enempilo ende kakhulu?

Ayikho impendulo yendawo yonke. Amandla, ukugeleza, izinto ezincintisanayo, ukwakhiwa, i-geometry yombhede kanye nendawo yokugcina indawo kufanele kuhlolwe iphrojekthi yangempela.

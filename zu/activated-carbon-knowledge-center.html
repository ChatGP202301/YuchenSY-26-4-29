Isikhungo Solwazi Sekhabhoni Esicushiwe

Funda ukuthi ikhabhoni ecushiwe ikhethwa kanjani embikweni wamanzi noma we-process-fluid, izinhlanganisela eziqondiwe, ukugeleza, izimo zokuxhumana, okokusebenza kanye nobufakazi. Yuchen Water inikezela ngezinto zekhabhoni ecushiwe namakhathriji futhi ihlinzeka ngosizo lobuchwepheshe lwemishini ehlobene. Lesi sikhungo sichaza indlela yesinqumo ngaphandle kokucela abathengi ukuthi bakhethe umkhiqizo kuqala.

Bika-inqubo yokuqala yokubuyekezwa kobuchwepheshe

  1. Thumela umbikoNikeza idatha yomthombo wamanzi noma i-process-fluid, izinhlanganisela eziqondiwe, ikhwalithi edingekayo yokukhipha, ukugeleza nezimo zokusebenza.
  2. Buyekeza ubungoziHlukanisa umsebenzi we-adsorption kusukela kokuqinile, amaminerali, ama-oxidants, izimo zebhayoloji nezinye izidingo zokwelashwa.
  3. Chaza isiqondiso sekhabhoniQhathanisa imidiya, GAC imikhumbi, CTO blocks, UDF cartridges, T33 polishing noma PAC dosing kuphela ngemva kokuba umsebenzi usucacile.
  4. Qondanisa izinto nezinto zokusebenzaQinisekisa ubufakazi obutholakalayo, ukucaciswa, indawo yokuhlala noma umkhumbi, ukwelashwa kusengaphambili, ukuqapha kanye nohlelo lwesevisi.
  5. Ukusebenza kokusekela nokushintshwaHlinzeka ngokuhlinzeka, ukubuyekezwa kwamadokhumenti, isiqondiso sobuchwepheshe esikude kanye nokuhlelwa kokumiselela ngaphakathi kwendawo okuvunyelwene ngayo.

Izindlela zohlelo lokusebenza

Umasipala namanzi okuphuza

Buyekeza ama-oxidants, ukunambitheka kanye nokukhathazeka ngephunga, izinto eziphilayo, ukugeleza kanye nobufakazi obuqondile obudingekayo kukhatriji ehlongozwayo noma uhlelo.

Amanzi angaphansi komhlaba namanzi omthombo

Hlola amaminerali, izinsimbi, i-turbidity, izinto eziphilayo nezimo zebhayoloji ngaphambi kokwabela indima kukhabhoni.

Amanzi enqubo yezimboni

Chaza inhlanganisela eqondiwe, ukuhlukahluka kwenqubo, izimo zokuxhumana, umsebenzi womkhumbi kanye nokuqapha okuphumelelayo.

RO kanye NF nokwelashwa kwangaphambili

Hlola ukuthi ikhabhoni ecushiwe iyabhekana yini ne-oxidant ebhaliwe noma ingozi yezinto eziphilayo nokuthi isiteji sizogcinwa kanjani.

Amanzi angcolile futhi usebenzise kabusha

Sebenzisa amakhemikhali okusakaza aphelele kanye nomlando wenqubo; ikhabhoni ingaba yisinyathelo esisodwa esitimeleni esikhulu sokwelapha.

OEM kanye nezihlungi esikhundleni

Ukwakhiwa komdlalo, ubukhulu, ukuxhumana, ukugeleza, ubufakazi nohlelo lwesevisi esikhundleni sokukhetha kulebula yodwa.

Housing, pump and system compatibility

Checking a membrane OEM replacement against the whole system

A buyer searching for a membrane OEM replacement is usually trying to solve a fit and performance problem. A 600 GPD, 800 GPD or 1000 GPD label does not answer that problem by itself.

Physical fit

Check element length and diameter, end geometry, port locations, seal position and the housing interface. Similar flow ratings can use different physical arrangements.

Hydraulic fit

Review the pump's operating pressure and available flow, not only the pump nameplate. The membrane, housing and flow path must work together at the intended operating point.

Water and duty

Feed water temperature, salinity, pretreatment, target permeate flow, rejection and recovery all affect the result. Send the operating conditions with the model inquiry.

For an existing system: send the full membrane label, clear housing photos and pump information. For a new design, add the housing drawing, pump curve and target operating point.

Product verification and traceability

The label, technical sheet and order records need to agree

A photograph can show the product in hand. Traceability comes from checking that evidence against the paperwork used to buy, quote and deliver the same model.

Model and packaging: compare the complete model, sealed bag and carton presentation.

Identifier and batch trail: check the QR or barcode and keep batch information with the order file.

Commercial records: connect purchasing, quotation and delivery records to the supplied model and quantity.

Questions buyers ask before ordering

Manufacturer, supplier and compatibility questions

Does Yuchen manufacture Vontron membranes?

No. Vontron is the membrane manufacturer. These Vontron elements are not made in a Yuchen Water factory. Yuchen acts as the independent supplier and handles model checks, compatibility review and export delivery.

Are you an authorized Vontron distributor?

We do not claim to be an authorized Vontron distributor. Buyers comparing a reverse osmosis membrane element company in China should distinguish the manufacturer from the company supplying and documenting the order. Vontron manufactures the element. Yuchen supplies it independently.

Can you supply traceable original Vontron products?

Yes. We supply original VONTRON membranes in VONTRON's original packaging. We check the model label, packaging, QR or barcode, batch information, and purchase-to-delivery records. OEM/ODM customization applies to water-treatment equipment and systems, not replacement branding of the original membrane packaging. Send your membrane model and system requirements for a quotation.

Can you support special Vontron model requirements?

Yes, after technical review. The 3213-800CQ on this page is a special model supply example. Quantity, manufacturer confirmation and the final specification must be agreed before supply.

What information is required for a custom or special model?

Send the existing membrane model, housing dimensions and interface, pump pressure, feed water information, target flow and rejection, order quantity, destination country, and any required certification or documentation.

Does an 800 GPD rating guarantee 800 GPD in my system?

No. GPD is measured under stated test conditions. Actual output changes with pressure, temperature, feed water chemistry, recovery, pretreatment and system design.

How do I confirm housing and pump compatibility?

Check the membrane length and diameter, port and seal arrangement, housing geometry, pump operating pressure, feed conditions, target flow and recovery before ordering.

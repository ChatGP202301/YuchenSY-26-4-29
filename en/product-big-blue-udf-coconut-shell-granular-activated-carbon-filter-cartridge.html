Big Blue UDF Coconut-Shell Granular Activated Carbon Filter Cartridge

Product overview

The Big Blue UDF Coconut-Shell Granular Activated Carbon Filter Cartridge uses a big blue cartridge. It uses Coconut-shell granular activated carbon (GAC/UDF). It is intended for an adsorption stage in household water purifier and replacement-filter programs.

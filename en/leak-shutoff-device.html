Leak shutoff device

Leak protection

This non-electric leak shutoff is designed to interrupt the purifier’s water supply when water reaches its trigger. Its two tube ports connect it in the feed line. Select the connection size against the existing tube before specifying the device for a cabinet installation.

Function
Water-triggered shutoff, as described by the supplier
Power supply
No electrical connection required
Connections
Two tube ports; listed connection sizes 1/4″ and 3/8″
Enquiry checklist

Choosing a serviceable installation

Identify the feed tube that needs to be isolated and record its outside diameter. Leave access to the device for inspection after a leak. Before specifying it for a machine, obtain the model’s activation and reset instructions, including whether the disc must be replaced after use.

What to send for a quote

Send the purifier model, a feed-line photo, measured tube outside diameter and requested quantity. Ask for the trigger and reset instructions, the disc’s function, whether it is replaced after activation and how many are supplied.

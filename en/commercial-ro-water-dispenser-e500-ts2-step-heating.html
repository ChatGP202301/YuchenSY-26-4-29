Key Features

E500-TS2 Step-Heating Desktop RO Water Dispenser

  • Compact desktop design for small and medium commercial spaces.
  • A 13 L step-heating system supports higher hot-water demand.
  • Integrated filter cartridges simplify service access.
  • The 200G configuration uses a 2-gallon pressure tank and is listed for 20–50 users.
  • The tempered-glass touch panel is easy to operate and clean.

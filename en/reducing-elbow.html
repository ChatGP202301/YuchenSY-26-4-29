Push-fit reducing elbow

Tube fittings

A 90-degree elbow that turns a threaded filter connection into a push-fit connection for PE tubing. Two versions share the same nominal threaded-end size and differ at the tube end.

Versions shown

Enquiry checklist

Selecting the tube end

Choose the elbow against the outside diameter marked on the tube. The two versions look similar from the threaded side. Include the tube marking and the mating port specification on the same enquiry so the supplier can identify both ends.

What to send for a quote

Send the mating threaded-port specification, a photo of the PE tube marking or its measured outside diameter, the desired elbow version and the quantity. Confirm the thread standard before ordering.

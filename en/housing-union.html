Filter housing union adapter

Filter housing connections

Connect the threaded head of a large-body filter housing to PP-R pipe while retaining a detachable service joint. Once the pipe-side socket is heat-fused, the union nut provides a separation point for removing the housing head.

Housing end
1-inch male thread
Pipe end
PP-R heat-fusion socket, supplier designation 25
Joint
Removable union with a separate sealing ring shown
Enquiry checklist

Matching the housing port

Start with the port on the filter head. Several BB-style housings are available with different port sizes and thread constructions under the same housing name. Use the port specification for the exact head being ordered. On the pipe side, match the fusion socket to the pipe marking.

What to send for a quote

Send the housing model and a close-up of its port, the marked PP-R pipe size or measured outside diameter, and the required quantity. Confirm the thread standard and whether the nut and seal are included.

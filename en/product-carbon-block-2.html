Buyer selection notes

Compressed Activated CTO Carbon Block is normally compared by carbon media grade, adsorption target, micron rating, cartridge length, cap style and housing fit. For private label water filter cartridge programs, the useful quotation details are wrapper design, replacement-set mix, carton language and pallet packing.

Details to confirm before quotation

  • Carbon media grade, iodine value and target taste issue
  • Micron rating, length, pressure drop and compatible housing
  • End cap, gasket, wrapper and replacement-kit design
  • Private-label artwork, carton language and pallet plan

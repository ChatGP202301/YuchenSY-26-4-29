Integrated Quick-Connect PP Sediment Filter Cartridge

The Integrated Quick-Connect PP Sediment Filter Cartridge is a prefiltration cartridge for household water purification equipment. It provides coarse filtration of raw water and is described as filtering sediment, impurities, rust and suspended matter.

UDF

Integrated Quick-Connect UDF Coconut-Shell Granular Activated Carbon Filter Cartridge

The Integrated Quick-Connect UDF Coconut-Shell Granular Activated Carbon Filter Cartridge uses granular activated carbon described as adsorbing and reducing color, odor, residual chlorine, halogenated hydrogen compounds and organic matter in water.

CTO

Integrated Quick-Connect CTO Coconut-Shell Carbon Block Filter Cartridge

The Integrated Quick-Connect CTO Coconut-Shell Carbon Block Filter Cartridge is described as intercepting organic matter, particles and other impurities while adsorbing and reducing color, odor, residual chlorine and other radioactive substances. It is also described as it inhibits microbial reproduction, purifies water and improves taste.

RO

Integrated Quick-Connect RO Membrane Filter Cartridge

The Integrated Quick-Connect RO Membrane Filter Cartridge has a stated filtration precision of 0.001–0.0001 micrometres. The stated salt rejection rate is above 98%. It is described as it allows water molecules to pass while retaining pesticides, bacteria, viruses, heavy-metal ions and other harmful substances, and that the filtered water can be consumed directly.

T33

Integrated Quick-Connect Large T33 Coconut-Shell Activated Carbon Filter Cartridge

The Integrated Quick-Connect Large T33 Coconut-Shell Activated Carbon Filter Cartridge contains acid-washed coconut-shell activated carbon with a stated iodine value above 1100. Product information describes strong adsorption, reduction of color, odor and residual chlorine, increased oxygen content, inhibition of microbial growth and improved water taste.

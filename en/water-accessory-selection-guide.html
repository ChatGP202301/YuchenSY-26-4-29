Choosing connections and controls for water treatment

Filter housing union adapter

1″ ♂ → 25 PPR

Ask for the male thread standard, the outside diameter of the PP-R pipe to be fusion joined, the housing model and the parts supplied.

Push-fit reducing elbow

90° · 1″ → 1/2″ / 1″ → 3/8″

The left elbow is marked 1/2″ and the right elbow 3/8″. Record the tube outside diameter and thread standard separately for each version.

Inline pressure-reducing valve

1/4″ / 3/8″

Request the inlet and outlet sizes for each model, the permitted inlet pressure, the outlet setting and the flow direction.

Four-port valve

4 × 3/8″

Request the manufacturer's circuit drawing identifying every port, the tubing size and the condition that operates the valve.

Leak shutoff device

1/4″ / 3/8″

Confirm the connection sizes, trigger and reset procedure, and the purpose and supplied quantity of the wrapped disc.

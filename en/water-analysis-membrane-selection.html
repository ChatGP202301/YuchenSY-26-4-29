Technical buying paths

Seawater RO: membrane selection and system buying guide

From intake chemistry to SWRO, polishing and a seawater-equipment inquiry.

Industrial wastewater reuse: selecting fouling-resistant RO

Separate pretreatment, fouling-resistant RO and concentrate duties before assessing equipment feasibility.

Electronics ultrapure water: where RO fits

Define point-of-use requirements and the boundary between RO and the complete UPW train.

For seawater include intake and seasonal chemistry. For reuse include process chemicals, upstream treatment and the concentrate outlet. For electronics include point-of-use analytical limits and the complete purification/distribution scope. Identify unknowns rather than supplying guessed values.

What to include in the technical brief

Share water chemistry, treatment goals and operating conditions so Yuchen Water can recommend a membrane category, material direction and related equipment support.

Without a representative report and confirmed operating conditions, any response is preliminary and is not a final material or performance commitment.

Start with your application, required product-water flow, project country or site, and contact details. A complete laboratory report is not required for an initial discussion: state “Water report not yet available” and send what you have. Mark measured values, estimates and missing data separately.

Do not submit passwords, confidential credentials or restricted personal data. A secure document link or direct email is preferred for the report.

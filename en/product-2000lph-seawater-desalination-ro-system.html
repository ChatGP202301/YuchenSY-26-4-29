Data required before a project quotation

  • source and intake method
  • salinity, conductivity, TDS and temperature range
  • complete major-ion analysis and boron where relevant
  • turbidity, suspended solids and silt-density information
  • organic and microbiological indicators

  • target product-water quality and daily output
  • existing pretreatment, pressure equipment and concentrate constraints

FAQ

Is 2,000 L/h guaranteed for every seawater source?

No. It is the nominal configuration label. Feed chemistry, intake conditions, temperature, product-water target and operating conditions must be reviewed.

Which components are fixed in the documented configuration?

The page records the Lingxiao CMI8-35(T) shared pump, 2465 sand filter, two 30-inch security filters, MF101 dosing, BOTO high-pressure pump, four SW8040LE-400 elements, PLC controls and 200 L flushing/CIP tank.

What must be sent before quotation?

Send a representative water analysis, intake details, required product-water quality, operating schedule, power supply, site layout and concentrate-discharge conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers for OEM/ODM water purification buyers: certifications, MOQ, lead time, RO systems, filter media, global shipping and after-sales support.

MOQ for standard 10" PP, CTO and GAC cartridges starts at 1,000 pcs per SKU for stock items, 3,000 pcs for OEM-printed sleeves, and 5,000 pcs for fully customized ODM moldings (private end-cap, custom O-ring, branded shrink-wrap). UF membranes and RO 75/100/400 GPD membranes typically MOQ 500 pcs. Wall-mounted dispensers MOQ 100 units. Smaller trial orders are negotiable for new distributors.

Our manufacturing center is in Yuanhua Town, Haining City, Zhejiang Province, China — approximately 90 minutes by car from Shanghai Hongqiao Airport (SHA) and Hangzhou Xiaoshan Airport (HGH). The 20,000+ m² facility houses dedicated PP melt-blown extrusion lines, sintered carbon block presses, UF/RO membrane assembly clean rooms, and dispenser SMT/assembly lines. We welcome scheduled buyer visits, third-party inspections (SGS, BV, TÜV) and OEM qualification audits.

Standard PP, CTO, GAC and T33 cartridges in stock colors ship within 7–10 days. OEM private-label cartridges with custom printing: 20–25 days from artwork approval. RO/UF membrane elements: 15–20 days. Wall-mounted and desktop dispensers: 30–45 days depending on customization. Container loading typically 1 × 20'GP (≈ 80,000 cartridges) or 1 × 40'HQ (≈ 200 dispensers). FOB Shanghai or Ningbo; CIF, CFR and DDP terms available.

We offer full-range reverse osmosis membranes: 50 GPD, 75 GPD, 100 GPD, 200 GPD, 400 GPD, 600 GPD for under-sink residential systems, plus 300 GPD, 400 GPD and 600 GPD high-rejection elements for light commercial water dispensers. Industrial 4040 and 8040 elements (BW30, SW30 equivalents) are available for water treatment plants, dialysis, semiconductor rinse and pharmaceutical applications. Salt rejection rate ≥ 96–99%.

PP melt-blown sediment filters use thermally bonded polypropylene fibers to retain suspended particles such as rust, sand and silt at the specified micron rating. CTO carbon blocks use compressed activated carbon for taste-and-odor and chlorine-related applications, while GAC cartridges use loose carbon granules where contact time and flow requirements suit the system. Claims for chloramine, VOC, pesticide, lead, mercury, PFOA/PFOS or other specific compounds apply only when supported by documentation for the stated sample, conditions and capacity; review our selected carbon-filter test data before requesting supporting files.

Key Features

E300 Commercial RO Water Dispenser

  • A 5 L or 10 L hot tank and 100G or 400G RO configuration can be selected.
  • The LED display shows TDS, temperature and filter-replacement reminders.
  • Four temperature settings and an automatic sleep mode support energy management.
  • One-touch dispensing provides separate hot and ambient outlets.
  • A child lock is provided for the hot-water outlet.
  • The cabinet uses 0.8 mm galvanized steel and stainless steel with protective coating.
  • A built-in 3.2-gallon pressure tank can support one or two branch dispensers.

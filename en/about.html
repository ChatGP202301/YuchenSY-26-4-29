About Us

Founded in 1998, Yuchen Water manufactures water filtration products for distributors and OEM buyers in more than 50 countries. Product-specific quality documents and selected test data are reviewed against the requested model and market.

Our Story

27+ Years Producing Premium Water Filtration

Founded in 1998 in Yuanhua Town, Haining City, Zhejiang Province — about 90 minutes from Shanghai's container ports — Yuchen Water works as an OEM water filter supplier and ODM water purifier manufacturer for distributors, importers and appliance brands.

Our 20,000+ m² facility with ISO 9001 quality management documents available upon request houses dedicated production lines for PP melt-blown sediment filters, sintered CTO carbon block filters, GAC granular carbon, T33 post-carbon, UF hollow-fiber membranes, and the full RO membrane element family from 50 GPD residential to 600 GPD commercial.

Today Yuchen Water supplies buyers in more than 50 countries. Product documentation is confirmed by model, destination market and order requirement.

Direct answer

What is the H-35?

The H-35 is a commercial RO water dispenser, with documented dimensions of 1100 × 475 × 1500 mm and a documented output of 200 L/h. The listed configuration is source-traced, while the final production specification must be confirmed for the buyer's water, electrical and application requirements.

Application

Where does it fit?

This dispenser family is intended for shared commercial drinking-water areas. Site traffic, daily demand, temperature functions, local electrical standard and feed-water quality determine the suitable configuration.

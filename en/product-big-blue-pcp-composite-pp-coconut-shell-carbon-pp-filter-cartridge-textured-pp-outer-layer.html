Big Blue PCP Composite PP–Coconut-Shell Carbon–PP Filter Cartridge – Textured PP Outer Layer

Product overview

The Big Blue PCP Composite PP–Coconut-Shell Carbon–PP Filter Cartridge – Textured PP Outer Layer uses a big blue cartridge. It uses Polypropylene and coconut-shell activated carbon. Its composite construction combines sediment and activated-carbon media within a Big Blue cartridge format. This version is identified by its textured pp outer layer.

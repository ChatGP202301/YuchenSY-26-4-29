Flat-End UDF Coconut-Shell Granular Activated Carbon Filter Cartridge – Blue End Caps

Product overview

The Flat-End UDF Coconut-Shell Granular Activated Carbon Filter Cartridge – Blue End Caps uses a flat-end cartridge. It uses Coconut-shell granular activated carbon (GAC/UDF). It is intended for an adsorption stage in household water purifier and replacement-filter programs. This version is identified by its blue end caps.

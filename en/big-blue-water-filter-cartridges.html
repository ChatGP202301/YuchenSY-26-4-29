Big Blue PP Sediment Filter Cartridge

The Big Blue PP Sediment Filter Cartridge is a prefiltration cartridge for household water purification equipment. It provides coarse filtration of raw water and is described as filtering sediment, impurities, rust and suspended matter.

PP

Big Blue Grooved PP Sediment Filter Cartridge

The Big Blue Grooved PP Sediment Filter Cartridge is a prefiltration cartridge for household water purification equipment. It provides coarse filtration of raw water and is described as filtering sediment, impurities, rust and suspended matter. The grooved construction increases the PP filtration surface area.

UDF

Big Blue UDF Coconut-Shell Granular Activated Carbon Filter Cartridge

The Big Blue UDF Coconut-Shell Granular Activated Carbon Filter Cartridge uses granular activated carbon described as adsorbing and reducing color, odor, residual chlorine, halogenated hydrogen compounds and organic matter in water.

CTO

Big Blue CTO Carbon Block Filter Cartridge

The Big Blue CTO Carbon Block Filter Cartridge is described as intercepting organic matter, particles and other impurities while adsorbing and reducing color, odor, residual chlorine and other radioactive substances. It is also described as it inhibits microbial reproduction, purifies water and improves taste.

Big Blue PCP Composite PP–Coconut-Shell Carbon–PP Filter Cartridge

The Big Blue PCP Composite PP–Coconut-Shell Carbon–PP Filter Cartridge combines multiple PP layers with high-iodine, acid-washed coconut-shell activated carbon. The PP layers are described as intercepting fine particles for coarse filtration, while the activated carbon reduces color, odor, residual chlorine and other harmful chemical substances to improve taste.

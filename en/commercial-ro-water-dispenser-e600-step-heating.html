Key Features

E600 Step-Heating Commercial RO Water Dispenser

  • The removable front glass panel is designed for easier service access.
  • A 100G or 400G RO configuration can be selected.
  • The drain tray can use a direct-drain or removable arrangement.
  • The 20 L step-heating system heats in stages without mixed or dry-boiled water.
  • The inner tank uses SUS 304 stainless steel with polyurethane foam insulation.
  • The touch control displays TDS, temperature, countdown and filter reminders.
  • A built-in 3.2-gallon pressure tank can support one or two branch dispensers.
  • Separate hot and ambient outlets are provided, with a child lock for hot water.

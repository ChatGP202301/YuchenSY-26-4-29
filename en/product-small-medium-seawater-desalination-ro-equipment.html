Buyer selection notes

Small and Medium Seawater Desalination RO Equipment is specified around seawater salinity, intake conditions, target output, high-pressure pump selection, membrane array, recovery rate and corrosion-resistant components. For coastal or marine projects, these details are more important than a broad reverse osmosis plant manufacturer claim.

Details to confirm before quotation

  • Salinity range, intake water condition and target output
  • Pretreatment, high-pressure pump and membrane array
  • Recovery rate, power supply and corrosion-resistance needs
  • Frame, container, spare parts and coastal-project documents

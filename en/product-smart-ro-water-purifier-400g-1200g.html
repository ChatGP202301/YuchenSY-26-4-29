UNDERSTAND THE FILTER OPTIONS

How reverse osmosis filtration works

These are general component roles, not a verified flow diagram of the photographed 800G sample. Up to 9 stages can be specified; media, sequence and component matching are confirmed for each project.

PP sediment filtration

Polypropylene media intercept suspended particles such as sediment and rust. Particle retention depends on the selected cartridge specification; PP does not remove dissolved salts as a membrane does.

Granular activated carbon / UDF

Loose activated-carbon granules provide adsorption and are commonly used to reduce chlorine taste and odour. UDF is a common cartridge designation; the supplied media and tested performance require confirmation.

Carbon block / CTO

Bound or compressed activated carbon forms a block rather than a loose granular bed. Chlorine taste and odour reduction and particle retention depend on the cartridge specification and operating conditions.

RO membrane and pressure

The selected pump supplies pressure for the membrane configuration. Reverse osmosis separates feed water into permeate (product water) and concentrate, which leaves through a separate outlet. Actual output depends on the membrane, feed water and system design.

Post-carbon / T33

A post-carbon cartridge can refine taste and odour after RO treatment. T33 commonly describes an inline post-carbon cartridge. When the terms identify the same cartridge, it counts once.

Optional mineralisation

Selected mineral media can change the mineral composition and taste of RO permeate. The result depends on the media, flow and contact conditions; no health benefit is claimed.

Coconut-shell carbon and iodine number

Coconut shell identifies a carbon raw material, not an additional filtration stage. Iodine number is a carbon characterisation measure from a defined adsorption test. A high iodine number alone does not establish removal of a particular contaminant or the performance of the complete purifier.

Questions buyers ask

What should I include in a wholesale RO water purifier inquiry?

Send your destination market, target capacity, estimated quantity, membrane and pump preferences, cartridge requirements and branding needs. We confirm configuration and MOQ before quotation.

Can I specify a RO water filter with TDS display and mineralisation?

TDS display, filter replacement reminders, T33 post-carbon and mineralisation are project options. Their functions and compatibility are confirmed with the selected configuration.

How do I select a replacement cartridge for this under sink RO system?

Confirm the cartridge dimensions, connections and replacement model for your configuration. A standard cartridge description alone does not establish compatibility.

Can you supply an under sink RO water purifier for private label?

Yes. OEM branding, panel language, housing, manuals and packaging can be discussed for the selected model and order.

Which capacities can I request for an under sink RO water purifier?

The project range can include 100, 400, 600, 800, 1200 or 1600 GPD. Confirm the requested configuration with the sales team.

No. It is the photographed 800G reference sample. Other capacities and internal configurations are confirmed separately.

No. The platform supports up to 9 stages. Media, sequence and the actual number of stages depend on the project.

Are the replacement filters universally compatible?

Standard cartridge options are available, but cartridge dimensions, interfaces and replacement models must be matched to the selected configuration.

Can I specify my own membrane, pump and panel language?

Yes. Include these requirements in your OEM inquiry. The final component selection and panel functions are confirmed during specification review.

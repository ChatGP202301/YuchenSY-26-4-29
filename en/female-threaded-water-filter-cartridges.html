PP

Female-Threaded PP Sediment Filter Cartridge

The Female-Threaded PP Sediment Filter Cartridge is a prefiltration cartridge for household water purification equipment. It provides coarse filtration of raw water and is described as filtering sediment, impurities, rust and suspended matter.

UDF

Female-Threaded UDF Coconut-Shell Granular Activated Carbon Filter Cartridge

The Female-Threaded UDF Coconut-Shell Granular Activated Carbon Filter Cartridge uses granular activated carbon described as adsorbing and reducing color, odor, residual chlorine, halogenated hydrogen compounds and organic matter in water.

CTO

Female-Threaded CTO Carbon Block Filter Cartridge

The Female-Threaded CTO Carbon Block Filter Cartridge is described as intercepting organic matter, particles and other impurities while adsorbing and reducing color, odor, residual chlorine and other radioactive substances. It is also described as it inhibits microbial reproduction, purifies water and improves taste.

T33

Female-Threaded T33 Coconut-Shell Activated Carbon Filter Cartridge

The Female-Threaded T33 Coconut-Shell Activated Carbon Filter Cartridge contains coconut-shell activated carbon with a stated iodine value above 900. Product information describes strong adsorption, reduction of color, odor and residual chlorine, increased oxygen content, inhibition of microbial growth and improved water taste.

T33

Female-Threaded Small T33 Coconut-Shell Activated Carbon Filter Cartridge

The Female-Threaded Small T33 Coconut-Shell Activated Carbon Filter Cartridge contains acid-washed coconut-shell activated carbon with a stated iodine value above 1100. Product information describes strong adsorption, reduction of color, odor and residual chlorine, increased oxygen content, inhibition of microbial growth and improved water taste.

Four-port valve

Water purifier valves

A four-port valve for purifier tubing, with a 3/8″ connection at each port. When replacing an existing valve, the four line destinations and the valve’s switching function determine whether the replacement belongs in the same circuit.

Connections
Four ports
Port markings
3/8″ at each of the four ports
Port assignment
Model-specific circuit drawing required
Enquiry checklist

Replacing a four-port valve

Photograph the existing installation before disconnecting any tube. Trace each line to its next component and submit the valve model and circuit drawing. Confirm each port’s role and what causes the valve to switch.

What to send for a quote

Send the valve model, photos of all four connections and the manufacturer’s circuit drawing. Mark where each tube goes and describe when the valve should switch.

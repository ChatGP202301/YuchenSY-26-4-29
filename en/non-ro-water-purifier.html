Non-RO Drinking Water Purifiers

Direct answer

A non-RO water purifier does not use a reverse-osmosis membrane as its primary treatment stage. It may combine mechanical filtration, activated carbon, UV treatment or functional media, so selection needs to match the water source, installation and maintenance conditions.

How to compare configurations

Select a non-RO system by the actual water source and treatment sequence, not only by the number of stages. A UV model requires confirmation of electrical supply and lamp-service access, while mineral or alkaline configurations require written confirmation of the media and its position in the system.

Fact and performance boundaries

These units should not automatically be treated as RO systems or used to support unspecified contaminant-reduction claims. The quotation should distinguish mechanical filtration, activated carbon, the UV stage and any functional media so the buyer can compare the supplied configuration.

Preparing a quotation

For a comparable quotation, state the water source, intended use, required treatment sequence, user count and installation location. For a UV configuration, include the available voltage and lamp-service access; for an alkaline or mineral system, confirm the required media. Also provide quantity, destination and packaging requirements so the supplied stages can be identified without assuming RO treatment.

For quotation, provide the water source, required treatment stages, electrical supply for UV models, user count, quantity and destination.

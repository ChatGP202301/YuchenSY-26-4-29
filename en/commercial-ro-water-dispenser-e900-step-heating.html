Key Features

E900 Step-Heating Commercial RO Water Dispenser

  • The removable front glass panel is designed for easier service access.
  • A 400G, 600G or 800G RO configuration can be selected.
  • The drain tray uses a direct-drain arrangement.
  • The 40 L step-heating system heats in stages without mixed or dry-boiled water.
  • The inner tank uses SUS 304 stainless steel with polyurethane foam insulation.
  • The touch control displays TDS, temperature, countdown and filter reminders.
  • A built-in 6-gallon pressure tank can support multiple branch dispensers.
  • Separate hot and ambient outlets are provided, with a child lock for hot water.
  • This configuration combines 20-inch filters, a 40 L hot tank and a 6-gallon pressure tank.

Key Features

E500-S Commercial RO Water Dispenser

  • A 5 L or 10 L hot tank can be selected.
  • The LED display shows TDS, temperature and filter-replacement reminders.
  • Four temperature settings and an automatic sleep mode support energy management.
  • The inner tank uses SUS 304 stainless steel with automated welding.
  • The cabinet uses 0.8 mm galvanized steel with a protective coating.
  • A stainless-steel ambient-water tank.
  • A 3.2-gallon pressure tank can be configured for branch dispensing.

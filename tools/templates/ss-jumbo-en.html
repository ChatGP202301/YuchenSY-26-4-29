Selection and maintenance

Before quotation, confirm cartridge length and diameter, the required connection, installation space, pressure-gauge requirement and access for cartridge replacement. During maintenance, isolate the water supply, release pressure and inspect the clamp and seals.

Confirm your housing configuration

This page covers SUS 304 housings for 10- and 20-inch Jumbo / Big Blue cartridges, with an optional pressure gauge. Photos show different head and connection arrangements; confirm the supplied configuration before ordering.

For a quotation, send the cartridge length and diameter, connection size and thread standard, operating pressure and temperature, available installation space and required quantity. Housing dimensions, seal material, pressure rating and the supplied accessories require confirmation.
